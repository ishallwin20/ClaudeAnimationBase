# Hanuman Ji series: his whole life in 10 standalone reels

## Context

Approved by the owner on 11 Nov 2026.

The owner wants a longer Painted series that covers Hanuman Ji's whole life, mixing known and little-known stories,
where every part can go viral alone (the bar: Sindoor, ad 29, and "forgot his powers", ad 60). The owner's condition
on the first draft: **every part uses solid sourcing.** So no "legend every dadi knows" episodes in this series: each
beat comes from Valmiki, the Mahabharata, the Bhagavata, the Shiva Purana or the Ramcharitmanas, and each part below
was checked against the text on 11 Nov (what the text does not say is listed, so it stays off screen).

Nine Hanuman stories are already posted (sun swallow, curse, sindoor, Sanjeevani ×2, leap, Lanka burns, Panchmukhi,
Bhima's tail, pearls). The series does not retell them; where the life story passes one, the episode recaps it in
5–8 s with the existing rigs and moves on to something new.

## Series rules

- **Style:** Painted, Bangers captions, retro SFX, no VO, music only as mild synth beds under the tender beats.
- **Tone:** adorable first (the Sindoor/curse toolkit: heart eyes, blush, emotes, poofs, cute foley), one emotional
  beat per part. Parts 6 and 9 are the "power" episodes: shoot them as cinema (build-up, silence, impact).
- **Shape (Parshurama Part 6 pacing):** frame-0 hook with one subject → cold open (≤10 s) → about 15 caption groups
  (≥3.5 s for 2 lines, ≥4.5 s for 3) → twist → ask → card. 60–90 s.
- **Standalone:** no cliffhangers. A small "HANUMAN · PART N" pill and the cover's big PART N are the only series
  marks; the last caption before the card teases the next part in one line.
- **Sources:** one named text per part (two only where the hook is the difference between them, Part 1). The source
  is named on screen in a small line when it is not Valmiki (Shiva Purana, Ramcharitmanas, Mahabharata, Bhagavata).
  Step 1 of every build is re-reading the chapter in a verse-numbered edition and writing "Facts used" with verse
  numbers; the 11 Nov check used prose translations, which give chapters but mostly not verses.
- **Card:** the no-price Navadurga card (`navCard`) with follow; confirm the offer line before Part 1's card.
- **Ask:** identity asks (name, place, a person), not quizzes.

Cut after the owner's review ("fewer parts is fine, each must be solid"): the school on the Sun (once checked, Valmiki
has only that he travelled from the rising hill to the setting hill with a book: no walking backwards, no fee; too
thin for a reel, so it lives on as Surya's boon in Part 2) and Bharat's fire vow (Hanuman is only the messenger).

Corrections the source check forced (keep them off screen): "this embrace is all I have" is a paraphrase of Yuddha 1
(Part 4); Part 4's "it wasn't her" beat is at the start of Sundara 11, to be read at build; Part 6 must not give a
reason for Ravana's failure; Part 7 must not explain the headless arrow.

Left out for lack of a solid text: his own Ramayan thrown in the sea, the empty seat at Ram Katha, Shani and the
oil, Akbar and the Chalisa, the tail throne, the ring in the netherworld, Suvarchala.

Build order 1 → 10. Parts 5 and 6 have the strongest hooks; if a faster test is wanted, build Part 5 first.

## How each episode is built

Branch `ad/<n>-books123-hanuman-<k>-<slug>` off `main` (Part 1 = ad 85). Per episode:

1. Re-read the source with verse numbers; write `STORYBOARD.md` (hook, beats with times, caption groups, "Facts
   used", ask, pinned comment).
2. Scene `src/scenes/hanuK.js` + `hanuK_props.js`, SFX `tools/hanuK_sfx.mjs`, `src/config.js`, `studio.html`.
3. Stills check (frame 0 first), then one render with SFX after clearing `out/frames`.
4. Cover with a big PART N, Instagram caption and pinned comment, all in `out/hanuK_out/`.
5. Commit; shared rig changes go to `main`. Ask the owner for the result before starting the next.

Reused as they are:
- Cast on `main`: `src/bal_hanuman.js` (Parts 1–2), `src/hanuman.js` (3–10), `src/ram.js`, Sita via `parvati()` +
  `SITA_PAL`, `src/jambavan.js`, `src/ravana.js`, `src/vayu.js`, `src/surya.js`, `src/indra.js`, `src/yama.js`,
  `src/shiva.js`, `src/vishnu.js`, `src/krishna.js`, `arjuna()` in `src/dashavatar.js`, `src/ramayan.js` and
  `src/ramayan_sets.js` (Brahma, Ayodhya's court, Lanka sets).
- `navCard` from `src/scenes/parshu2_props.js` and the PART N cover from `src/scenes/parshu8_cover.js` (ad/80 branch).
- The cute SFX set in `tools/curse_sfx.mjs` (ad/60).

Ram's rig (Part 7).

## Verification

  cut before drawing.
- `node render.mjs --stills=0` for the frame-0 hook, then stills at each caption group to check the safe band
  (y 420–1500, x < 930) and read times.
- Full render, watch once at speed, check loudness (about −16 LUFS).
- Deliverables in `out/hanuK_out/`: `hanuK.mp4`, `cover.png`, `captions.txt`.
