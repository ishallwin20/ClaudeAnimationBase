# Ayyappa series: Swami Ayyappa's story in 5 standalone reels

## Context

Approved by the owner on 18 Nov 2026: "a small series on Ayyappa for core followers", "accurate sources, southern
sources if possible". The Sabarimala season (Mandala–Makaravilakku) opened in mid-November 2026, so millions of
devotees are on the 41-day vratam while the series posts; aim to finish before Makaravilakku (mid-January 2027).

## Sources (checked 18 Nov 2026)

The Ayyappa story is not in the great Sanskrit puranas. Its texts are southern, and the series uses them by name:

1. **Bhoothanathopakhyanam** (ശ്രീ ഭൂതനാഥോപാഖ്യാനം), the Malayalam kilippattu that set the story Sabarimala tells:
   printed in 1929 (Kallarakkal Krishnan Kartha), out of print after 1947, reprinted by the Sabari Sharanasramam Trust.
   Framed as Suta telling the rishis at Naimisha. Ten chapters: 1–6 the story, 7–10 the Bhutanatha Gita (advaita).
   **Read in** the Janmabhumi daily's chapter-by-chapter prose rendering (2014), mirrored at
   hinduwayoflife2016.blogspot.com/2017/11/1.html … /6.html (parts 18–23). It is a prose retelling with the key
   verses quoted, not the verse text: when a beat hangs on wording, quote it as "the Bhoothanathopakhyanam tells".
   - ch. 1: Chandika kills Mahishasura; his sister **Mahishi** goes to Shukra, does tapas on the Vindhya on her right
     big toe, eyes on the sun, and is about to burn herself when Brahma appears ("any boon but deathlessness"). Her
     boon: nothing in the world (stone, wood, fire, water, wind, weapons) and no disease may harm her; Indra and the
     devas lose to her; she rules heaven; buffaloes like her spring from every pore; **only a son born of Hari and Hara
     who has lived twelve years on earth as a human's servant may kill her.** She takes heaven, cuts the mandara trees of
     Nandana. Brihaspati tells her past: she was **Leela**, Dattatreya's wife, cursed by him to be born a she-buffalo
     ("mahishi") after she blocked his way to tapas crying "I am your mahishi (queen)"; she cursed him back. Born to
     Karambha with a buffalo's face (Mahishasura is Rambha's son, her cousin; the text calls her his sister). Vishnu sends
     **Sundara-mahisha** (the trimurti's amsha, Datta reborn, a ruby bell made by Vishvakarma at his neck) to lure her
     to earth.
   - ch. 2: Durvasa's garland and curse (the devas grow old), the churning; Dhanvantari and the amrit; the asuras seize
     it; Vishnu as **Mohini** offers to share it, then sets a test: **"close your eyes; the one who opens them LAST I will
     marry"**, and serves the devas while they wait; one asura peeks, finds her gone; the devas, young again, beat them.
   - ch. 3: Shiva hears of Mohini, asks Vishnu to show him the form; Vishnu vanishes; a garden; under a **champak**
     tree Mohini plays with a **ruby ball**; she runs, he catches her; from them a radiant form is born, Dharma Shasta,
     "to fulfil the Pandalam king's fortune and to end Mahishi's pride"; born on the last day of Dhanu (Makara
     Sankranti), a Saturday, in Uttram; grown at once, blue (nila), eight-armed. **Shiva embraces him; Vishnu leaves
     the Mohini form and embraces him; Parvati and Lakshmi feed him divine milk;** Saraswati settles on his tongue;
     Brahma gives him a thousand names (Bhutanatha, Dharma Shasta, Parayagupta, Aryatata, with meanings). Vishnu tells
     him to live with Shiva on Kailasa; he learns every art from Shiva.
   - ch. 4: Shiva: "to end Mahishi you must live twelve years among humans: go to the forest by the Pampa, lie there as
     a crying baby; the Pandalam king will come". **Shiva puts a golden bell at his throat: so, Manikanta.** King
     Rajasekhara hunts, rests by the Pampa, hears a baby crying, finds a child shining like a thousand suns; Vishnu as an
     old brahmin: "take him; in twelve years you will know who he is; see the gold bell: call him Manikanta".
   - ch. 5: his schooling (the khadga, the horse); his guru-dakshina: he heals the guru's blind and mute son; the
     queen bears her own son, Rajarajan; Manikanta lives as the king's servant; at twelve the king plans to crown him
     yuvaraja in 30 days; **the minister** (the villain) tries sorcery (Shiva comes as a sannyasi and heals him), then
     poison (Garuda drops amrit in the food), then talks **the queen** into a fake headache and a bribed vaidya who
     prescribes **tigress's milk**; the king won't send him; the boy makes him promise, goes alone with a bundle and a
     three-eyed coconut; women of the court tease him: "bring us tiger cubs to play with!" "I will." He reaches the
     Pampa; the bhutas (Vaparan, Kaduravan, Veerabhadra…) gather; the rishis build Ponnambalam for him.
   - ch. 6: Mahishi leaves Sundara-mahisha and storms heaven again; the devas pray to him at the Pampa; he catches her by
     the horns, whirls her and throws her down by the Azhutha (Alasa) river, **dances on her**; she praises him and
     is freed: Leela rises in a vimana and asks to be his; **"in this life I cannot leave brahmacharya; stay as my sister,
     Manjamata, a little to my left"** (Malikappurathamma); Shiva sends him to Pandalam and tells him **Shabari, who
     waited for Ram, was reborn and meditates on the Nila mountain east of the Pampa: give her moksha; the hill will be
     called Sabarimala; the Pandalam king will build your temple there**; Brahma: bury Mahishi's body east of the
     Azhutha under stones (**Kallidamkunnu**); **Indra becomes a tiger, the devis tigresses, the devas tiger cubs**,
     and he rides into Pandalam; the people flee; the queen was cured the day he left; the king knows him for a god;
     Agastya tells him the whole story.
2. **Kanda Puranam** (கந்த புராணம்), Kachiyappa Sivachariyar (Tamil, about the 14th century), Asura Kandam,
   **Maha Sastha Padalam** (படலம் 32, 68 verses). Read in Arumuga Navalar's prose (vasanam) edition, 1953 printing
   (archive.org, "Kandha Puranam Vasanam 1953"). Indra tells Indrani at Sirkazhi who will guard her: the churning; Mohini
   (here the asuras fight each other for her and die); Rahu and Ketu; **Shiva comes "to show that Vishnu is one of his
   four shaktis"**; Mohini, shy: "you have Uma… men do not unite with men"; Shiva: "you are my shakti; you became a
   woman at Darukavana; you bore Brahma from your navel"; she runs; under a **sal tree** on the northern shore of
   Jambudvipa they unite; the Gandaki's shaligrams come from that day; **a son is born, dark, with red matted hair,
   holding a chendu; Shiva names him Harihara-putra**, makes him one of the Rudras and lord of a world. Indra then calls
   Ayyanar, who comes on a white elephant with Purna and Pushkala and sets Mahakala to guard Indrani (Ajamukhi's
   episode follows). Note: the Kanda Puranam's Ayyanar has two consorts; Sabarimala's Ayyappa is a naishtika
   brahmachari. Cite the Kanda Puranam for the birth and the name only.
3. **Bhagavata Purana 8.12** (vedabase.io): Shiva asks Vishnu to show the form that bewildered the asuras (8.12.12–14);
   in a garden he sees a woman playing with a ball (8.12.18); he follows her (8.12.27); where his seed fell, gold and
   silver mines (8.12.33). **No child**: the Bhagavata has the chase, not Ayyappa. Use it only as "the Bhagavata tells
   the garden and the ball too".
4. **Sabarimala practice** (Travancore Devaswom Board, the pilgrim customs): the 41-day vratam, the mala, black or
   blue clothes, "Swami" for everyone, the irumudi, the 18 steps, the arrows at Sharamkuthi, the stones at
   Kallidamkunnu, the Thiruvabharanam from Pandalam, Harivarasanam at closing, "Tat Tvam Asi" above the sannidhanam.
   These are customs, shown as customs, never as plot.

## Series rules

- **Style:** Painted, Bangers captions, retro SFX, no VO, mild synth beds only under the tender beats (as the Hanuman
  series).
- **Tone:** adorable first (little Manikanta, the bell, the tiger cubs), one emotional beat per part.
- **Shape:** frame-0 hook with one subject → cold open → about 15 caption groups (≥3.5 s for 2 lines, ≥4.5 s for 3)
  → twist → ask → card. 70–90 s.
- **Marks:** a small "AYYAPPA · PART N" pill and the source line beside it in the opening only (about 9 s); the cover's
  big PART N. A text is named in a caption whenever a beat leans on it.
- **Card:** the no-price Navadurga card (`hsCard`) with follow; confirm the offer line with the owner.
- **Ask:** identity asks (what your family calls him, your first year, your temple), not quizzes.
- **Stay off:** the women's-entry case, the Makaravilakku lighting dispute, any claim that Ayyappa = Ayyanar (the
  Kanda Puranam is cited for the birth and the name only), Vavar's backstory (the text has a bhuta named Vaparan;
  the Erumeli tradition differs: if Vavar ever comes in, show the practice, not a story).
