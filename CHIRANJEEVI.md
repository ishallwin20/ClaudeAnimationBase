# CHIRANJEEVI: a phone-first comic series (Rishi Katha Comics, Season 1)

Approved by the owner on 14 Nov 2026. This file is the season plan; read it first when asked to build an issue.

## Context

Rishi Katha sells live wallpapers (impulse, ₹21 bundles) and physical kids' books (bought by parents). The Instagram
audience, though, is mostly 18–34, and what they respond to is clear from the reels: "known yet unknown" stories,
strict sourcing, series (Hanuman, Parshurama, Ramayan), identity asks, myth-vs-text explainers. Nothing we sell is made
for *them* to read themselves. The owner wants to test an ebook product for this group (a graphic comic, 10–12 books,
easy to share) and asked for a solid plan plus my own ideas.

My view in one paragraph: **yes, but don't make a print comic squeezed onto a phone.** Make a comic *designed for the
phone*: 4:5 "swipe pages" (Instagram's carousel ratio), 1–3 panels each, every swipe a page-turn reveal. One master
then becomes everything at once: the IG carousel (marketing), the PDF (WhatsApp-shareable), a fixed-layout EPUB (Google
Play Books / Kindle), a web reader on rishikatha.com (with read-through analytics) and later a printed omnibus. And
**run it as an experiment with gates**: build Issue 1 to the highest bar, launch it free with an "Issue 2" sign-up,
measure for ~10 days, then commit to the season (or not). Don't build 12 before the market says yes.

**Owner's decisions (14 Nov 2026):** Season 1 = CHIRANJEEVI. Art look: **Inked** (owner, 14 Nov, after the style test;
see COMIC_GUIDE.md). After the thumbnail read (14 Nov): **no Season Pass**; each issue is priced on its own and Issue 1
is complimentary (the owner makes deals and bundles later, on the website). **English only**; Hindi later.

## 2. The series: CHIRANJEEVI, "8 people who never died"

*Ashwatthama Balir Vyaso Hanumanash cha Vibhishanah, Kripah Parashuramash cha saptaite chiranjivinah; Markandeyam
athashtamam.* A famous shloka most people half-know (known yet unknown), a big 18–34 pull since *Kalki 2898 AD* made
Ashwatthama a household name, standalone issues with a season arc, and a sourced finale: Bhagavata 8.13 names several of
them as Saptarishis of the next manvantara, and the Kalki Purana has Parshurama teach Kalki.

## 3. The look

A comic needs what a reel doesn't: the same faces in 100+ panels, close-ups of eyes and hands, many poses. That is the
**biggest risk** with code-made art (rigs repeat poses), so the pilot tests it before anything else.

- Base it on **Cinematic** (realistic proportions, film light, the largest adult cast: Ravana, Yama, Markandeya, Shiva,
  Vamana, Sita, Angad, Jatayu…). Painted reads as kids/comedy (and the owner has said Painted isn't for books).
- Test two looks on the same 3 pages: **(A) Cinematic as it is**, and **(B) "Inked"**: a new comic pass on cine.js
  (variable-weight ink outline, two-tone cel shade, halftone in the shadows, speed lines). B is a new named style
  (COMIC_GUIDE.md, STYLES.md row) only if the owner picks it.
- Panel craft that hides rig limits: strong silhouettes, dedicated close-up drawings (eyes, hands, the gem), symbolic
  splash panels, environment-led panels, 3D-engine establishing shots (Kurukshetra at night, Lanka, Kailash).
- Lettering: comic balloons drawn in SVG with tails to the speaker; dialogue font **Kalam** (SIL OFL, Latin +
  Devanagari, so the Hindi edition uses the same face), Bangers for SFX, Marcellus for titles.
- No AI images anywhere (the IG carousel and trailer reels stay label-free).

## 4. The pipeline (in this repo)

Modelled on `tools/book.mjs` (art from the engine, words from a data file, live text, PDF + fixed-layout EPUB).

- `comics/<slug>/SCRIPT.md`: the script, page by page and panel by panel, plus "Facts used" with verse numbers.
- `comics/<slug>/comic.js`: `COMIC = { pages: [{ layout, panels: [{ rect, balloons: [{ text, at, tail, kind }], captions, sfx }] }] }`;
  shared by the art page (panel rects) and the tool (lettering). A `lang` map holds the Hindi text.
- `src/scenes/<slug>_comic.js` + `comic.html`: one slot per swipe page (t = page + .5, as book.mjs does), the scene
  draws each panel clipped to its rect with its own camera.
- `tools/comic.mjs` (new; reuses book.mjs's crop, PDF and EPUB code): `--comic=comics/<slug> [--pages=] [--render]
  [--lang=hi] [--thumbs]` writes `out/comics/<slug>_out/`: `<slug>.pdf`, `<slug>.epub` (pre-paginated, single pages,
  no spreads), `<slug>.cbz`, `carousel/01..20.png` (Issue 0 / previews), `cover.png`, `wallpaper.png`, and `web/`
  (images + manifest.json for the site's reader). `--thumbs` = grey-box art + real lettering for a pacing read.
- Every page carries a small @therishikatha watermark; the purchased PDF can carry a "This copy belongs to <name>"
  page (social DRM, and makes gifting nice).
- Branches: engine and tool on `main`; each issue on `comic/<n>-<slug>`; the season plan in `CHIRANJEEVI.md` on main
  (like HANUMAN_SERIES.md).
- Site: hand-off `instructions.md` + `manifest.json` for the website repo's agent (as with the wallpaper batches): a
  vertical-scroll reader with per-page analytics, free/paid gating, an "Issue 2" sign-up.

## 5. Launch funnel (Issue 1)

1. **Issue 0 carousel** on IG (20 slides, the eight immortals), last slide "Issue 1 is out: comment AMAR / link in bio".
2. **Trailer reel** from Issue 1's panels (Cinematic camera moves over the art, Lyria bed), as a trial reel too.
3. First 10 pages of Issue 1 as a second carousel, ending on a cliff.
4. Issue 1 free on the site (web reader + PDF); last page → "tell me when Issue 2 is out" sign-up.
5. Before launch: 10 followers beta-read Issue 1 on their phones (feedback + first testimonials).

## 6. Experiment gates (owner to tune the numbers)

| Measure | Where | Good | Kill / rethink |
|---|---|---|---|
| Issue 0 saves + shares | IG insights | above the account's carousel/reel median | far below |
| Issue 1 reads | site | ≥ 3,000 in 10 days | < 500 |
| Read-through (reached last page) | web reader | ≥ 40% | < 20% → issues too long: try 15-page "shorts" |
| Issue 2 sign-ups | site | ≥ 5% of readers | < 1% |
| Paid conversion (Issue 2 sales, once out) | Razorpay | ≥ 2% of Issue 1 readers | < 0.5% → keep comics free as a funnel for books/wallpapers |

## 7. Timeline (sessions, one thing per session as with the reel series)

1. Write `CHIRANJEEVI.md` (series plan) and Issue 1's `SCRIPT.md` from the Sauptika/Drona/Adi Parva text with verses. Owner reviews the script.
2. Build `tools/comic.mjs` + `comic.html` + lettering; style test: Issue 1 pp. 1–3 (the owl and the crows) in look A and B. Owner picks on the phone.
3. Thumbnail pass of all ~40 pages (`--thumbs`) → owner reads it on the phone for pacing.
4–5. Final art and lettering; outputs (English; Hindi later).
6. Issue 0 carousel, trailer reel, cover, captions, site hand-off.
Then launch, ~10 days of numbers, the gate. If go: an issue every ~2 weeks between reels.

## 8. Risks

- **Pose variety in code art** → the pilot tests it first; design around close-ups and silhouettes; add rig poses on main.
- **Time taken from reels** (reels are the engine of everything) → an issue ≈ 4–5 sessions; gates stop the sink early.
- **Accuracy backlash** → retelling rules from CLAUDE.md apply, verse-numbered "Facts used" on every issue; folk claims
  (e.g., Ashwatthama sightings) only inside the "what the texts don't say" box.
- **Piracy of the PDF** → accepted: a free-to-spread Issue 1 is the marketing; paid copies stamped with the buyer's name.
- **Store rules** (Play Books / Kindle / Apple in India) → verify before phase 2; launch on our own site only.
