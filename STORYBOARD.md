# "NEVER offer THIS flower to SHIVA" (ad 66 · Books 1–3 · 3D)

A 72.5 s reel in the **3D** style, #2 of the five-reel push (31 Oct–1 Nov 2026), in the shape of ads 50/54: a ritual
almost every Hindu does (offering flowers), a "have I been doing it wrong?" turn, and sources on screen. The hook is the
ketaki, a flower that once lied. Then the Shiva Purana's story of the pillar of fire (ad 43's void), the second flower it
bars (champa), and the twist that many will argue about: the same Purana LISTS tulsi for Shiva. Then the god who really
refuses tulsi (Ganesha) and the god who loves both of Shiva's barred flowers (Vishnu). Then the screenshot board, a
YES/NO ask and next: Rudrabhishek (#3 of the push). English VO (Sarvam `girish_en_devotional`, pace 1.2), karaoke
captions, SFX under it (music added on Instagram). Matte: no metal on the flowers, no fx bloom, the linga's stone less
polished (owner, 30 Oct: ad 64's Pushpak was too shiny). Card: Books 1–3, set of 3 for ₹500.

Performance only (no claim): the studio on black, the void and the orbs (ad 43's), the flower meeting Brahma in the
fire, the red light on "LIE", the flower falling away, the courtyard (ad 50's), the floating flowers, the lightning on
"cursed", the marks, the water on the linga for "next".

Not used (unsourced): akshata for Vishnu, durva for Devi, bilva for Surya (the verse *nākṣatair arcayed viṣṇuṃ…* has no
source we could find); conch water for Shiva (only blogs).

## Voiceover (tools/never_vo.json; captions are the script)
```
hook     NEVER offer THIS flower to SHIVA.
lie      It once told a LIE.
fight    BRAHMA and VISHNU fought: who is greater?
pillar   Between them rose a PILLAR OF FIRE with no end.
search   Vishnu dived for its ROOT. / Brahma flew up to find the TOP.
fell     On the way, a flower fell from SHIVA'S HEAD: / the KETAKI.
beg      Brahma begged it: "Say I saw the top."
lied     And the flower LIED.
curse    Shiva said: "I have no desire / to include you in my worship."
champa   The SHIVA PURANA bars one more: CHAMPA.
tulsi    But TULSI? Many say: NEVER for Shiva.
lists    The Shiva Purana LISTS Tulsi for Shiva: / JOY in this life, and MOKSHA.
ganesha  The god who REFUSES Tulsi is GANESHA.
cursed   She wished to marry him. He said no. / They CURSED each other.
vishnu   And the two flowers Shiva REFUSES? / VISHNU loves them.
save     SCREENSHOT THIS before your next puja.
ask      Did you know Tulsi is ALLOWED for Shiva? / YES or NO? Comment!
next     NEXT: what you POUR on Shiva decides what you GET. / FOLLOW so you don't miss it.
```
Speech-to-text (saaras:v3) on every take, word for word after two fixes ("lists it… joy" was heard as "listed… join":
the TTS text says "lists Tulsi, for Shiva: joy, in this life"; "for its top" as "for its stop": now "to find the top").
