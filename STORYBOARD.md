# Bee breath with Bal Ganesha (Scribble style, 36 s seamless loop)

Part of the Scribble "Breathe with…" set (after reel/breathe-hanuman). Made only in code: no AI images, no photo.

## The loop
- Bhramari pranayama, three rounds of 12 s: **Breathe in** (4 s, "through your nose"), then **Hmmm** (8 s, "hum like a
  bee"). Frame 0 already shows "Breathe in"; the last frame ends the third hum, so Instagram's replay has no seam.
- Bal Ganesha sits on a pink lotus on a lily pad in a pond in the morning, thrown far out of focus. Eyes closed, hands in
  gyan mudra. Breathing in, his chest fills, his head lifts and his trunk curls up; while he hums his big ears flutter.
- Six honeybees circle him on tilted orbits (passing behind and in front of his head). They swing wide on the in-breath
  and draw in close while he hums, trembling with the sound.
- The words: "Breathe in" rises and grows; "Hmmm" grows an m at a time and its letters tremble while the hum lasts.
- Sound (`tools/beebreath_sfx.mjs` → `assets/beebreath_sfx.wav`): a tanpura on Sa, a soft breath in through the nose, a
  hummed "mmm" on Sa with the low Sa under it, a small swarm joining in, a singing bowl at each in-breath. Loops.

## Facts used
- Bhramari: Hatha Yoga Pradipika 2.68: breathe in quickly with a sound like a male bee, breathe out slowly humming like a
  female bee (bhṛṅganāda / bhṛṅgīnāda). The reel teaches the simpler version most teachers use today (a quiet in-breath,
  a long hum); the caption says so. The name is from bhramara, a bee.
- Ganesha: the broken tusk is his left one (Ekadanta), as in the shared cast. Nothing in the reel claims Ganesha taught
  or practised Bhramari: he simply hums along.
- No medical claims; the caption says to stop if dizzy.
