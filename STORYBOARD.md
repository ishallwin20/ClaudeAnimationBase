# "Narad Muni thought he looked like VISHNU." (ad 22 · Narada moha · 15K Family Deal card)

A ~102 s captioned Painted reel (Bangers captions, synthesized SFX, no voiceover, music added on Instagram). A follower
sent the plot; this is the director's cut of it, told as the Ramcharitmanas tells it. Hook: the monkey face first, then
the payoff that makes it matter: his curse over that face became the Ramayana.

## Facts used (Ramcharitmanas, Bal Kand, dohas 125–139 (Gita Press); the same story in the Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Srishti Khanda 2–4)
- Narada sat in tapas in a Himalayan cave by the Ganga. Indra feared Narada wanted his throne and sent Kamadeva with
  spring to break it. Kama's arts failed: "who can trespass on one whose great guardian is Ramapati (Vishnu)?" Kama
  fell at Narada's feet; Narada felt no anger and spoke kindly to him. (Manas 125–126)
- Proud of beating Kama, Narada told Shiva. Shiva begged him: "never tell this to Hari as you told me." Narada didn't
  like the advice and went (by way of Brahma, left out) straight to Vishnu and told him. (127–128)
- Vishnu saw "a great tree of pride sprouting in his heart" and resolved to uproot it, for Narada's own good. (129)
- On Narada's way, Vishnu's maya built a great city; King Shilanidhi's daughter Vishwamohini was holding a swayamvara.
  Narada read her palm: whoever weds her becomes immortal and none can defeat him. He forgot his detachment. (129–131)
- He prayed to Hari: "give me your form (beauty)". Vishnu appeared and said: "I will do what is truly best for you,
  nothing else; my word is never false", and made his face a monkey's. Narada, under the maya, didn't know. (132–133)
- Two of Shiva's ganas sat by him in brahmin disguise and mocked him: "Hari has given him fine beauty! The princess will
  choose him, taking him for Hari." She saw the monkey face, was angered, and never looked his way; Narada kept rising
  up again and again. Vishnu came in a king's form; she garlanded him. (133–135)
- The ganas told him to look at his face; he saw it in water, and cursed them to be born as rakshasas. He looked in the
  water again and had his own form back, but his anger didn't cool. (135–136)
- He met Vishnu on the road with Lakshmi and the princess, and cursed him: take the (human) body you cheated me in; you
  gave me a monkey's form, so monkeys will be your helpers; as you hurt me, you will suffer separation from your wife.
  Vishnu accepted the curse gladly and withdrew his maya: neither Lakshmi nor the princess was there. Narada fell at
  his feet: "let my curse be false"; Vishnu: "it was my will". (136–138) The Manas gives this as the cause of Ram's
  birth in one kalpa.
- Language fact: in Sanskrit "hari" means Vishnu and also a monkey (Monier-Williams); the ganas' jibe ("taking him for
  Hari") rides on it.
- Performance only (no plot claim): a bee landing on his nose during the tapas, Kama's arrows pinging off a blue glow
  (the Manas's "guardian"), the "tree of pride" drawn as a sapling on his topknot that grows and finally drops, Shiva's
  facepalm, the swoon, the whack-a-mole popping, the scream, the ganas' horns peeking from their turbans.
