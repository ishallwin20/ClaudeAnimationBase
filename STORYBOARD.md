# "Some SADHUS never cut their hair. Some SHAVE it all off. WHY?" (ad 87 · 3D)

A 70.4 s explainer in the **3D** style (owner, 12 Nov 2026: "a 3D explainer on why some sadhus keep jata and some are
clean shaven; make it accurate"). English VO (Sarvam `girish_en_devotional`, pace 1.15), karaoke captions, SFX under it
(music added on Instagram), the Navadurga card (no price). Matte look: no fx bloom, cine.js bloom .12, DOF, a warm grade.

**The idea:** the answer is in the texts, and it's simple: they describe two roads out of the world. The forest hermit
(vanaprastha) never cuts; the wandering renouncer (sannyasi) shaves it all. The same split is alive today (Nagas keep
jata like Shiva, Dandi sannyasis stay shaven), with one twist (a Naga's initiation starts with a shaved head) and one
warning (Bhaja Govindam: matted or shaven, if it's for the stomach it's a costume). The ask is a place call-out.
First 3D reel with full figures: two matte figurines built from primitives, eyes closed, whose hair grows and is shaved.

## Facts used
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| The forest hermit "must wear jata, beard and nails uncut" | Manusmriti 6.6: *jaṭāś ca bibhṛyān nityaṃ śmaśru-loma-nakhāni ca* (the vanaprastha; G. Jha: "he shall not have cut") ([wisdomlib](https://de.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/manusmriti-with-the-commentary-of-medhatithi/d/doc200563.html)). |
| When Ram left for the forest he asked for banyan milk and matted his own hair | Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 52: *jaṭāḥ kṛtvā gamiṣyāmi nyagrodha-kṣīram ānaya*; "Guha immediately brought that latex… Rama made matted hair to himself and to Lakshmana" ([valmikiramayan.net, sarga 52](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga52/ayodhya_52_prose.htm)). Verse numbers differ by edition, so the tag says only "Ayodhya Kanda 52" |
| The wandering sannyasi owns nothing; his head is shaven | Jabala Upanishad 5: the parivrajaka is *muṇḍo 'parigrahaḥ* ("shaven, non-possession", tr. Radhakrishnan) ([text](https://bhagavadgitausa.com/JabalaUpanisad.htm)). Manusmriti 6.52 has the renouncer's hair, nails and beard clipped (*kḷpta-keśa-nakha-śmaśruḥ*) ([wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/manusmriti-with-the-commentary-of-medhatithi/d/doc200613.html)) |
| Even the shikha is given up | Jabala Upanishad 6 (paramahamsas renounce "tuft of hair and sacred thread… in water"); Narada-Parivrajaka Upanishad III ("after shaving the head together with the tuft of hair, cast off the holy thread") ([tr. Aiyar](https://neonvagabond.xyz/mirrors/sacred-texts/hin/tmu/tmu23.htm)) |
| Shiva is "the matted-haired" | Rig Veda 1.114.1 calls Rudra *kapardin*; Sayana glosses it *jaṭila* ([wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/rig-veda-english-translation/d/doc830234.html)) |
| Many who follow Shiva keep jata, like the Naga sadhus; Dandi sannyasis stay shaven | living practice: Nagas with matted hair and ash, Dashanami dandis of the path of study "often shaven-headed" ([Harvard CSWR, Sadhus](https://cswr.hds.harvard.edu/news/2026/03/27/sadhus)); the Narada-Parivrajaka Upanishad (VII) gives staff-carrying renouncers a regular shave by the seasons. Said as "many", not "all" |
| Even a Naga begins with a shaved head on the day of his diksha | Naga initiation at the Kumbh: mundan, then pind daan for oneself ([Deccan Herald, 2025](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/uttar-pradesh/maha-kumbh-2025-process-to-initiate-naga-sadhus-in-various-akharas-begins-3362908), [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/process-to-initiate-naga-sadhus-in-various-akharas-begins-at-maha-kumbh-2025/2452819/)) |
| Bhaja Govindam: matted or shaven, if only for the stomach it's a costume | *jaṭilo muṇḍī luñchitakeśaḥ kāṣāyāmbara-bahukṛta-veṣaḥ … udara-nimittaṃ bahukṛta-veṣaḥ* (verse 14, or 15 in some editions; by tradition Totakacharya's verse in Shankara's hymn) ([Kamakoti](https://kamakoti.org/shlokas/kshlok19.htm)) |
| "Both threw away the same thing: the comb" | our reading, not a verse: the householder's groomed hair is what both give up (the argument of P. Olivelle, "Hair and Society"). No source tag is shown on this beat |

Not said (kept out on purpose): that every Shaiva keeps jata or every Vaishnava shaves (Ramanandi tyagis keep jata,
many Vaishnava monks shave); that jata "stores energy" or shaving "opens a chakra"; Jain kesh-loch and Buddhist tonsure
(other traditions); the Narada-Parivrajaka's four kinds of sannyasi (the Hamsa wears jata there: too much for one reel,
it's in the pinned comment).

## Voiceover (tools/jata_vo.json; captions are the script)
```
hook    Some SADHUS | never cut their hair. / Some SHAVE | it all off. | WHY?
two     Because our old texts | describe TWO ROADS | out of the world.
hermit  Road one: | the forest HERMIT. / MANU says | he must wear JATA, | beard and nails uncut.
ram     So when RAM | left for the forest, / he asked for | BANYAN MILK / and matted | his own hair.
wander  Road two: | the wandering SANNYASI. / He owns nothing. | His head is SHAVEN. / Even the SHIKHA | is given up.
same    One never cuts. | One cuts it all. / Both threw away | the same thing: | the COMB.
today   Today, many who follow SHIVA, | the matted-haired, / keep JATA, | like the NAGA sadhus. / DANDI sannyasis, | staff in hand, | stay SHAVEN.
twist   But even a NAGA | begins with a SHAVED HEAD, / on the day | of his DIKSHA.
bhaja   And BHAJA GOVINDAM | says: / Matted or shaven, | if it's only for the stomach, / it's just a COSTUME.
ask     Where did YOU last see | a sadhu with JATA? / Comment the PLACE!
```
Spoken (tts): saadhus, jataa, Raam, sannyaasi, shikhaa, Naaga, Dandee, deekshaa. "warns" came back from speech-to-text
as "burns", so the line says "says". Speech-to-text (Sarvam saarika:v2.5) on every take: all right.

## Shots (times from the voice: src/scenes/jata_tl.js)
- **A 0–4.4 hook.** A banyan's two-tier stone platform on a river bank at dawn: the jata sadhu low and near (knot,
  locks, beard, ash, a mala; his dhuni burning), the shaven sannyasi higher behind (ochre robe, bamboo danda,
  kamandalu). Frame 0 is finished. The focus goes from one head to the other on SHAVE.
- **B –8.4 two roads.** A crane back and up over both; TWO ROADS.
- **C –15.4 the hermit.** Green forest light, an arc round the jata sadhu; from MANU his hair grows from stubble to
  full locks, the knot and the beard; Manu 6.6 in Devanagari and English.
- **D –22.3 Ram.** The banyan's trunk: the bow leaning on it, a cut branch dripping white latex into a leaf cup; the
  verse from Ayodhya Kanda 52.
- **E –29.6 the sannyasi.** An arc round him; he starts with a householder's hair and shikha: on SHAVEN the hair goes,
  on "given up" the shikha; मुण्डोऽपरिग्रहः.
- **F –35.6 the comb.** The two-shot, then a push down to a wooden comb left on the stone.
- **G –45.8 today.** Low on the jata sadhu with his trishul and fire: कपर्दिन् KAPARDIN, NAGA SADHU; then the
  sannyasi and his staff: DANDI SANNYASI.
- **H –50.5 the diksha.** Close on the Naga: on SHAVED HEAD his locks, knot and beard go; DIKSHA.
- **I –58 Bhaja Govindam.** Dusk, the two in silhouette against the river and the sun; the verse.
- **J –63.4 the ask.** Dawn again, the two-shot; WHICH PLACE?
- **Card –70.4.** The Navadurga card, no price.
