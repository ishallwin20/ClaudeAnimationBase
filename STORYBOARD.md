# Bappa ki 3 Complaints: storyboard

**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 32.7 s, 24 fps, silent. Everything is beat-locked at 120 bpm (one beat = 0.5 s), so trending audio added in Instagram lands on the hits.
**Caption safe zone:** captions sit between y = 260 and y = 1480 and x = 80 to 920. That keeps them clear of the reel's top bar, the bottom caption/username area and the right-hand like/share column. Captions use Permanent Marker, cream text on a thick ink stroke, and pop in on a beat.

**Logline:** Bappa wants people to celebrate him with love, but they keep celebrating their ego. He complains about three things, and for the last one he takes matters into his own hands (and trunk).

**World:** one riverside Mumbai-style ghat. Every shot happens along the same riverbank, so the reel feels like one place.
- Palette: marigold `#F2A93B`, saffron `#E8772E`, vermilion `#D2452F`, river teal `#3A9C98`, ghat stone `#C9B79C`, Bappa's skin a soft rose-peach `#F2B8A0`, ink `PAL.ink`, paper `PAL.paper`.
- Colour arc: warm festive dusk (hook) → harsh flat daylight with a chalky grey river (complaints) → golden hour with a clean blue river and marigold petals (outro).

**Motif:** the **modak**. Bappa holds it in the hook and is too annoyed to eat it. The problems keep interrupting him. He finally eats it in the last shot, once everything is fixed.

**Bappa's arc:** annoyed → exasperated → disgusted/sad → furious → idea → smug/relieved → happy.

**Cast:**
- **Bappa:** chibi and big-headed (the head is 60 % of his height). Rose-peach skin, huge ears, one `ribbon` trunk, a gold mukut, a yellow dhoti, a red dupatta and a round belly. He has two arms (keeping it simple and cute) and one broken tusk. His eyes and faces reuse the Clawd emotion system.
- **Mooshak:** a small grey mouse with a pink nose and a long tail (one `ribbon`). He mirrors Bappa's mood with a half-beat delay, which is where the comic timing comes from.

## Rule check
- **An event in every shot:** A has the pose copy and the finger, B the tilt and the reveal, C the splash and the cleanup, D the throw, E eating the modak. ✓
- **Reads don't overlap:** each caption gets at least 0.25 s per word plus a 0.4 s hold, and no caption lands during a big motion. The longest caption (#2, 10 words) gets 2.5 s. ✓
- **Transitions at every seam:** iris → match cut (the finger becomes the 1) → cut on action (the falling idol) → match cut (ripples become bass rings) → splash wipe → iris out. ✓
- **Text:** the user asked for captions. There's no other text, no signs on objects and no words on the speaker.
- **Ending rhymes with the opening:** the same close-up; the crossed arms and ignored modak become open arms and an eaten modak. ✓

## Tone notes
- The joke is always on the *practice*, never on devotees. Bappa is exasperated and loving, never mocking.
- The throw gag doesn't show any people getting hurt. The truck and DJ are off-frame; Bappa only takes the speaker tower.
