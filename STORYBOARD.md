# "DHANTERAS was never about GOLD" (ad 71 · Books 1–3 · 3D)

A 66.8 s reel in the **3D** style, built 1 Nov 2026 to post before Dhanteras (Fri 6 Nov 2026; Diwali Sun 8 Nov). It's
the owner's wife's idea ("Dhanteras is not about gold but health"), in the shape of ads 50/54/69: something almost every
family does this week (buying gold on Dhanteras), a "have I been doing it wrong?" hook at frame 0, sources on screen,
the screenshot board, a binary ask (GOLD or HEALTH?) that starts comment fights, and next: the Diwali aarti-along that ad
69 promised. English VO (Sarvam `girish_en_devotional`, pace 1.2), karaoke captions, SFX under it (music added on
Instagram). Matte (owner, 30 Oct): gold at metalness .6 / roughness .45, no fx bloom. Card: Navadurga Set, no price.

The turn: the gods churned the ocean for one thing, amrit. Wealth came up first (the treasures, then Lakshmi herself),
and they kept churning. The last to rise was Dhanvantari with the jug of amrit, the god of health. "Wealth came first.
Life was the prize." And the one rule the Padma Purana gives for this night is a lamp for Yama, with not one word about
gold.

Iconography, no claim: Dhanvantari as a dark bronze murti after 8.8.32–33 (dark-bodied, young, garlanded, ornamented,
yellow silk, pearl earrings, the jug); four arms with Vishnu's chakra and conch and a sprig of herbs, as his images show
him. Performance only: the coin spinning and falling, Mandara's forest, Vasuki's coil, the treasures as rising lights,
the lotus for Lakshmi, Dhanvantari rising from the milk, the village door, the rangoli, the coin on the step, the row
of lamps for next.

Checked and not used: "National Ayurveda Day is on Dhanteras": true 2016–2024, but from 2025 it's fixed on 23 September
(gazette, March 2025), so it's left out. "Buy new vessels because Dhanvantari held a pot": a common explanation, no
source found. Sushruta's "I am Dhanvantari, the first god" verse: only in the Nepalese manuscript, not the vulgate.

## Lessons
- In 9:16 the horizontal view is ~56% of the vertical: two subjects side by side at 60 m are both out of frame. Stack
  them in depth (one near and low, one far and tall) or move them in for that one shot.
- A flame sprite (additive) vanishes against a bright pool of its own light: keep a diya's point light ≤ .6 and frame
  it against dark ground.
- A cone mountain reads as a termite mound and a fat one as a dome: big low-frequency lobes, vertical gullies, crags
  at the top and instanced trees on the lower slopes give it scale.
- The voice tool's `hold` only lengthens a caption; to keep a board up longer, raise the next line's `gap`.
