## The series' rule here
The destroyers are never named on screen, in the voice or in the Instagram caption: only a year and a role, the name a
black bar. The raiders are faceless black shadows with no faith markers; no mosque on screen. Every rebuilder is named in
gold. Facts used (below) names everyone, for accuracy.

## Voice notes
STT (whisper small, en) heard "Ghats" as "gods/cart" in every take, also with Devanagari घाट; the voice says "ghaat"
(English STT doesn't know the word). "Widowed" came out mangled, so the line is "A widow at 29". "Build it" became
"Dilled it": Gandhi's line is "let the people pay for it, not the state". लिंग for "linga"; "Daksh", "Prabhas",
"Moolraaj", "Kumarpaal", "Mahipaal" spelled for the Hindi pronunciation.
