# Ad 64 · "The WHOLE Ramayan in ONE flight… told by Ram himself"

3D style in the film look (cine3d_india.js relief map, cine3d_fx.js), girish_en_devotional VO + SFX, 89.2 s. The
owner's idea (29 Oct 2026): trace the Ramayan on a map with the times Valmiki gives. The spine is Yuddha Kanda 123, where
Ram, flying home on the Pushpak, points out each place to Sita. Each place gets a pillar of light, Valmiki's name for it
with today's traditional site under it, and a memory from our Ramayan series (Parts 3–12) in a golden bubble tagged
PART N (clean, caption-free re-renders by `tools/pushpak_clips.mjs`). The time facts come from Valmiki too. The end card
(owner): the Parshurama series starts soon, our live wallpapers, follow. No book offer.

## Facts used (Valmiki Ramayana, valmikiramayan.net numbering)
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| After the war Ram flew home on the Pushpak and pointed out the places to Sita (Lanka on Trikuta, where Ravana fell; Nala's bridge; the sea where Mainaka rose for Hanuman to rest; Kishkindha, where he killed Vali; Rishyamuka, where he met Sugriva; Pampa, where he grieved and saw Shabari; where Jatayu fell, in Janasthana; Panchavati, Khara's army and the hut Sita was taken from; the Godavari; the ashrams of Agastya, Sutikshna, Sharabhanga, Atri, where Sita met Anasuya; Chitrakoot, where Bharata came; the Yamuna, Bharadvaja's ashram, the Ganga, Guha's Shringaverapura, the Sarayu, Ayodhya: "bow to it") | Yuddha 123 ([prose](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/yuddha/sarga123/yuddha_123_prose.htm)) |
| Bharata's vow: if Ram does not return when the 14 years end, he will enter the fire; Ram gives him his sandals | Ayodhya 112 ([sarga 112](https://valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga112/ayodhyaitrans112.htm)) |
| Bharata ruled from Nandigram with the sandals (the line at Chitrakoot: "His sandals ruled Ayodhya for 14 years") | Ayodhya 115; Yuddha 125 (Hanuman finds him at Nandigram with the sandals before him) |
| The bridge was built in 5 days | Yuddha 22.68–72 |
| Ram waited out the four rainy months at Prasravana by Kishkindha ("this is Shravana… four months of rain"; act in Kartika) | Kishkindha 26.14–17 ([sarga 26](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/kish/sarga26/kishkindharoman26.htm)) |
| Ten years among the ashrams of the Dandaka forest | Aranya 11 ([prose](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/aranya/sarga11/aranya_11_prose.htm)) |
| When the 14th year was complete, on Panchami, Ram reached Bharadvaja's ashram (the reel: "they landed") | Yuddha 124.1 ([sarga 124](https://valmikiramayan.net/utf8/yuddha/sarga124/yuddhaitrans124.htm)) |
| Panchami = the fifth day of the bright half; the reel shows a five-day-old waxing moon | the tithi itself (the month is not named in the verse; the reel names none) |
| Ram sent Hanuman ahead to Bharata (and Guha) to tell him of his return | Yuddha 125 ([prose](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/yuddha/sarga125/yuddha_125_prose.htm)) |

Identifications on screen are the traditional ones, not Valmiki's: Lanka = Sri Lanka, the Setu at Rameswaram,
Kishkindha / Rishyamuka / Pampa at Hampi, Panchavati at Nashik, Chitrakoot (UP/MP border), Prayag (Prayagraj),
Shringaverapura (Shringverpur), Nandigram by Ayodhya. Janasthana and Agastya's ashram get no modern name (contested:
Lepakshi, Taked, Akole…). The memories are our series' cartoons of the same beats (each part has its own Facts used).
Not claimed: the month of the return (Valmiki's Panchami has no month; no Diwali/Chaitra claim), any BCE date, any
distance in yojanas, how long the flight took.
