## Facts used (Devi Mahatmya / Durga Saptashati, as commonly retold)
- Mahishasura's boon: no god or man could kill him (he thought a woman no threat). He drove the gods from heaven.
- The gods' anger (their tejas) joined into one light, and Durga took form from it.
- Gifts: Shiva the trishul · Vishnu the chakra · Indra the vajra (and a bell) · Varuna the shankh (and a noose) · Vayu
  the bow and arrows · Kala the sword and shield · Yama a staff · the ocean a lotus garland · Himavan (the mountain king)
  the LION (her vahana) and jewels. (The gada in her hand is commonly shown; texts vary on its giver.)
- In battle he changed form: buffalo → lion → a man with a sword → elephant → buffalo; she pinned him with her foot as
  he came half out of the buffalo, and struck him down. (The swordsman form is folded into the final emergence.)
- Navratri's nine nights are popularly told as the nine nights of this battle; Vijayadashami ("the tenth day of
  victory") celebrates her win (also Rama's over Ravana).
