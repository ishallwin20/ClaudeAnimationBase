# "His father is SHIVA. His mother is VISHNU." (ad 101 · Ayyappa series Part 1 · Painted)

An 86.6 s reel in the **Painted** style, Bangers captions, retro SFX, no voice; the no-price Navadurga card. Part 1
of the Ayyappa series (AYYAPPA_SERIES.md), posted in the Sabarimala season (Mandala opened mid-November 2026).

**The idea:** the hook is the thing everyone half-knows and few can explain: Ayyappa is Harihara-putra, the son of
Shiva and of Vishnu as Mohini. The reel tells WHY he was born (Mahishi's boon), HOW (Mohini, the garden, the ball,
in Kerala's own text, the Bhoothanathopakhyanam, and Tamil Nadu's Kanda Puranam), and ends on the adorable twist the
Kerala text gives: both fathers hug him, and Parvati and Lakshmi both feed him milk. Then the gold bell that names him
Manikanta, and the Pampa, which is where Part 2 starts.

**Motif:** his bell. He rings it in the hook; it is missing at the birth; Shiva ties it on; he rings it as he lands by
the Pampa and twice in the ask. In the sound it is one small brass "ting".

## Facts used
Read on 18 Nov 2026. The sources and how each was read are in AYYAPPA_SERIES.md.

**Bhoothanathopakhyanam** (Malayalam kilippattu, 1929; read in the Janmabhumi prose serial, chapters 1–4):
- ch. 1: after Chandika kills Mahishasura, his sister Mahishi does tapas on the Vindhya for revenge on the devas,
  on one big toe with her eyes on the sun; Brahma appears ("any boon but deathlessness"). Her boon: nothing in the world
  and no disease may harm her, Indra and the devas lose to her, she rules heaven, buffaloes spring from her pores, and
  only a son born of Hari and Hara who has lived twelve years on earth as a human's servant may kill her. She takes
  heaven; Brihaspati: "she rules Amaravati; she has taken Indra's powers". On screen: "nothing could kill her, except a
  son of Shiva and Vishnu" (the twelve years come later, in the bell shot); "Indra lost his throne".
- ch. 2: (after Mahishi leaves heaven, and Durvasa's curse) the churning; the asuras seize the amrit; Vishnu appears
  among them as Mohini; asuras ask to marry her; her test: "close your eyes; I will serve the amrit; the one who opens
  his eyes last I will take as husband"; she serves the devas quickly; one asura opens his eyes and finds her and the pot
  gone. On screen: "later, the ocean was churned", "to win the amrit back from the asuras", the quote.
- ch. 3: Shiva hears of Mohini, goes to Vishnu: "I long to see that form"; Vishnu vanishes; a garden appears; under a
  champak (chempakam) tree Mohini plays with a ball made of ruby (manikyam); she runs, and at last he holds her; from
  them a radiant form appears (their sweat and Shiva's seed: told on screen only as "from the two of them, a child of
  light was born"); Shiva embraces the son; Vishnu leaves the Mohini form and embraces him; devas rain flowers;
  **Parvati and Lakshmi give him divine milk**; Saraswati settles on his tongue; Brahma gives him a thousand names,
  explaining four: Bhutanatha, Dharma Shasta, Parayagupta, Aryatata.
- ch. 4: Shiva: "you were born to end Mahishi's pride; for that you must live twelve years among humans; go to the
  forest by the Pampa, become a human baby"; he gives him a golden bell for his throat, and so he is called Manikanta.

**Kanda Puranam** (Tamil, Kachiyappa Sivachariyar), Asura Kandam, Maha Sastha Padalam (read in Navalar's prose
edition, 1953): Shiva and Vishnu as Mohini unite; a son is born, dark, red-haired, with a chendu; **Shiva names him
Harihara-putra** (அரிகரபுத்திரன்). On screen: "HARIHARA-PUTRA" with "son of Hari and Hara · Kanda Puranam (Tamil)".

**Bhagavata Purana 8.12** (vedabase.io): Shiva asks to see the form (12–14); a garden, a woman playing with a ball
(18); he follows her (27). No child: the reel cites the Bhagavata only for "the garden and the ball too".

Not claimed: Mahishi's past as Leela (Part 3 tells it), Sundara-mahisha and Durvasa (skipped: "later" covers the
gap); any reason Mahishi thought the boon safe (her laugh is performance); what Shiva and Mohini did beyond the chase
and the catch; that the Bhagavata has a child; that Ayyappa and the Kanda Puranam's Ayyanar are one (the reel quotes the
Kanda Puranam only for the birth and the name); the Kanda Puranam's details that differ (the sal tree, the asuras
fighting each other); the date of the birth (Makara Sankranti, Uttram: saved for a later part); his eight arms and
his growing to youth at once in ch. 3 (drawn as the series' little Manikanta throughout: a design choice).
Performance only: the bell flicks and giggles; the bubble (moon + chakra = ?); the cackle; the buffaloes popping in
round the throne; Indra slipping away on a cloud; heart eyes; Mohini's wink; the asuras' squeezing; one asura peeking
("one asura opens his eyes" is in ch. 2: shown as a peek); Surya standing beside Indra for "the devas"; Vishnu's wink;
the ball rolling away; the child hopping into Vishnu's lap; the milk moustache; "loved by all four" and its hearts; the
"+ 996 more" badge; the descent in a glow.
