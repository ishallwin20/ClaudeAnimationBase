# What happens if Shiva opens his third eye?

A 47.5-second vertical reel (1080×1920, 24 fps, bpm 90) for Rishi Katha's Navadurga picture books, starring Shiva,
Kamadeva (and his parrot) and Parvati. Scene: [src/scenes/third_eye_kama.js](src/scenes/third_eye_kama.js), sets and
props: [src/scenes/third_eye_kama_props.js](src/scenes/third_eye_kama_props.js), characters: [src/shiva.js](src/shiva.js),
[src/kamadeva.js](src/kamadeva.js), [src/parvati.js](src/parvati.js), brand images: `src/scenes/rk_images.js` (drawn with
`picture()`), sound effects: [tools/third_eye_kama_sfx.mjs](tools/third_eye_kama_sfx.mjs) → `assets/third_eye_kama_sfx.wav`.
No voiceover: captions carry it.

```
Logline: The gods need Shiva out of his meditation, so Kamadeva makes a false spring bloom on frozen Kailash and shoots
         a flower arrow at his heart. The third eye opens (his two eyes never do) and fire burns Kama and his illusion to
         ash. Not anger: the third eye sees what is real and burns what isn't. Kama lives on in every heart; Parvati
         chooses tapasya and becomes Maa Brahmacharini, Book 2.
Hook:    "WHAT HAPPENS IF SHIVA / OPENS HIS THIRD EYE?" is up on frame 0 over one simple picture: Shiva, eyes shut, a red
         ember in the closed third eye. It trembles and cracks, and we cut away on "One god found out the hard way."
World:   Kailash at night, ink blue and cold snow → Kama's false spring (pink sky, a green meadow, flowers, bees, too
         sweet) → red fire → the true snow, still and blue → truth-gold on the third eye → the brand peach.
Motifs:  The third eye: an ember (frame 0), fire (the strike), gold (the end). The eye-shaped seam: a red almond opens
         onto Kailash (A → B); a gold almond opens onto the books (H → card). The ending rhymes with the opening: the same
         close-up, now with his eyes open and smiling, and the third eye opens gold, not red.
Comment: "What would YOUR third eye burn? Tell us in the comments!" over Shiva's one-eyed peek at the camera.
Follow:  the card's last line is "Follow @therishikatha for Parvati's story".
Text:    every caption sits inside Instagram's safe band (y 420–1500, left of x 930).
```
