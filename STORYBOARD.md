# "Shani Dev's MOTHER was a SHADOW." (Shani series, Part 2 · ad 23 · 15K Family Deal card)

A ~93 s captioned Painted reel: no voiceover, synthesized SFX only (music added on Instagram), captions in **Bangers**
(emphasis is gold and size). Part 2 of the Shani series. Part 1 (ad 21, Ganesha's head) got the most interaction yet
because viewers felt for Shani; this one keeps that turn: he is the son of a stand-in mother, made to take another
woman's place and never tell. Part 3: "Ram's father aimed an arrow at Shani" (Padma Purana, Uttara Khanda).

## Facts used
Markandeya Purana chs. 77 and 103 (the story is told twice; ch. 103 adds Surya finding out through yoga), with the Vishnu
Purana 3.2 and the Harivamsha 1.9, which tell it the same way.
- Sanjna, Vishvakarma's daughter, is Surya's wife. She can't bear his blaze and shuts her eyes when she looks at him
  (MP 77). Her children by him: Vaivasvata Manu, and the twins Yama and Yami (Yami becomes the Yamuna river).
- She makes a woman out of her own shadow (Chhaya) to take her place, tells her to treat the children and Surya as she
  would and never tell him, and leaves for her father's house (and later does tapas in the north as a mare; not shown).
- Surya, thinking Chhaya is Sanjna, has three children by her: two sons, Savarni Manu and Shanaishchara (Shani), and a
  daughter, Tapati (the Tapti river).
- Chhaya loves her own children more than Sanjna's. Manu puts up with it; Yama doesn't. He raises his foot at her (he
  doesn't strike). She curses the foot to fall off.
- Yama tells Surya a mother wouldn't curse her own son. Surya finds the truth (through yoga, MP 103); afraid of his
  curse, Chhaya confesses.
- Surya goes to Vishvakarma, who trims his blaze on a lathe (MP 77, 103); from the trimmings Vishvakarma makes the gods'
  weapons, among them Vishnu's chakra and Shiva's trishul (Vishnu Purana 3.2: the hidden gag in the sparks). Surya then
  goes to bring Sanjna back.
- Shanaishchara means "the one who moves slowly" (shanaih, slowly + chara, moving). Saturn, the slowest of the planets
  seen by eye, takes about 29.5 years to go round the Sun, so it spends about 2.5 years in each of the 12 signs; Sade
  Sati is its passage through three of them (the 12th, 1st and 2nd from the natal Moon): 7.5 years.
- The Navagraha Stotra (attributed to Vyasa), Shani's verse: "nīlāñjana-samābhāsaṃ ravi-putraṃ yamāgrajam |
  chāyā-mārtaṇḍa-sambhūtaṃ taṃ namāmi śanaiścaram": "born of Chhaya and Martanda (the Sun)".
- Iconography: Shani's dark sapphire-blue skin (Matsya Purana), his crow (popular images).
- Performance only (no plot claim): Chhaya tossing laddoos to her favourites, Yama hopping on one foot, Manu's shrug,
  Surya oblivious with hearts, the little Shani holding his mother's saree as Surya leaves, and the closing image of
  Shani's shadow on the wall as his mother's (the stotra's line, shown).
- Not used: what became of Chhaya afterwards (the Puranas don't dwell on it), Surya rejecting Shani or Shani's gaze
  eclipsing him at birth (later tellings, not in these texts), the Ashvins' birth.
