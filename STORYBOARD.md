# Ramayan · Part 7: "Ravana Takes Sita" (ad 39 · the Ramayan series)

Part 7 of the 12-part Ramayan series (added so the story doesn't jump from the golden deer to Vali; format in ad 33's
storyboard). Painted, **no voice** (owner, 23 Oct: the chibi reels without a VO do best): Bangers captions carry the
story (timeline and captions in `src/scenes/rp7_tl.js`), the characters talk with moving mouths, and a retro cartoon SFX
track sits under it (about -16 LUFS, fixed gain); the only music is a soft tanpura and veena under the three sad scenes
(the empty hut, Jatayu dying, the pyre). 69.2 s. Made extra cute (owner, 23 Oct: "extremely cute, viral"): Ravana's ten
heads swoon with heart eyes, then drop their jaws at "lioness"; the chariot is pulled by donkeys (true: Aranya 49) and
they hee-haw; the monkeys go "ooh?!" as the bundle plops among them; the sad scenes stay tender. The hook: Sita dropped
her jewels from the sky; guess who found them. The twist: one of the five monkeys on the hill was Hanuman.

## Facts used (Valmiki Ramayana; valmikiramayan.net numbering)
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| Sita welcomes the mendicant as a guest; he reveals he is Ravana | Aranya 46; 47.26 ([sarga 47](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/aranya/sarga47/aranyasans47.htm)) |

| He takes his own form, seizes her; his golden chariot, yoked with donkeys ("PULLED BY… DONKEYS!"), rises into the sky | Aranya 49 |
| She cries out to the trees, the river (Godavari) and the deer to tell Ram | Aranya 49 |
| Jatayu, king of vultures, fights; Ravana cuts off his wings | Aranya 50–51 |
| Over a hill she sees five monkeys and drops her ornaments tied in her silk upper cloth; Ravana doesn't notice | Aranya 54 ([sarga 54](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/aranya/sarga54/aranyasans54.htm)) |
| Ram comes back to the empty hermitage and searches, calling her | Aranya 60–64 |
| He finds Jatayu dying; Jatayu tells him Ravana took her, and dies | Aranya 67–68 |
| Ram performs his funeral rites, as he would for his father | Aranya 68 |
| The five were Sugriva and his ministers; Hanuman among them; they found the jewels ("guess who found them?"), and Sugriva later shows them to Ram | Kishkindha 6 |
| The Part 8 tease: Ram meets Hanuman; Vali falls to an arrow from behind a tree | Kishkindha 3, 16 (Part 8) |

Not claimed: how Ravana held her (the text is graphic; the reel shows only the grab and the chariot); "Jatayu was
Dasharatha's friend" (true, Aranya 14, left out for time). Performance only: the "?" over the bundle, the fruit bowl, the poof and Ravana's laugh, the
heart eyes and hearts over his heads and their dropped jaws, the light behind Sita's retort, the donkeys' music note, the
brown deer looking up, the monkeys' "ooh?!", Hanuman's glow and sparkle with the bundle, his heart when he meets Ram.
