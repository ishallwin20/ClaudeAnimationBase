# "The FIRST to fight RAVANA for SITA wasn't Ram." Jatayu vs Ravana (Cinematic · Follow card · 76 s)

A captioned retelling in the **Cinematic** style ([CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md](CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md)) of Jatayu's fight with
Ravana, as the Valmiki Ramayana tells it (Aranya Kanda). There is no voice. The captions tell it, synthesized SFX carry
the hits, and a Lyria 3 Pro score sits underneath. It ends on a Follow card with no offer, as the Nandi reel does.

```
Logline: Jatayu, king of eagles and King Dasharatha's old friend, promised Ram he would guard Sita. When Ravana takes
         her in his gold chariot drawn by ghost-faced mules, she cries out to him; the old bird wakes. "I am old, you
         are young, armed, in a chariot. But you will not take her while I live." He snaps Ravana's bow, kills the
         mules and the charioteer, smashes the chariot, tears off ten of Ravana's arms (they grow back). Old and tired,
         he falls to Ravana's sword: his wings cut off. Sita weeps over him; Ravana flies off with her. Still alive when
         Ram comes, he tells him "south" and dies in Ram's arms. Ram lights his pyre as for his own father.
Why it should travel: a famous villain and a famous name with a hook that turns what everyone knows ("the first to
         fight for Sita wasn't Ram"), a surprising number (60,000 years), sourced shock details (ghost-faced mules,
         arms that grow back), an underdog who loses and is honoured, a silence at the cut, the emotional payoff
         (Ram as a son), a callback line ("He fought ANYWAY") and a place call-out.
Comment: "Is there a JATAYU temple near YOU? Comment your city!" (a place call-out, as the Shakti Peeth and Ramlila
         ones worked). Pinned: "Lepakshi, Chadayamangalam, Nashik… which Jatayu place have you visited? 🪶"
```

## Files
- Scene: `src/scenes/jatayu.js` (times in `TL`), props `src/scenes/jatayu_props.js` (the skies, Ravana's `rig` in the
  air, arrows and `volley`, the bow's halves and its pearls, the torn arms, falling feathers, the anklet's spark, rocks).
- New shared Cinematic cast (on `main`): `src/cine_jatayu.js` (`jatayu()` perch, spread, fallen; `jatayuHead()`,
  `jatayuTalon()`, `feather()`), `src/cine_sita.js` (`sita()` stand, carried, kneel), Ravana's `frontL`/`frontR`
  ('carry', 'sword', 'bow', 'fist'), `lostLeft`, `rvCarryArm()`, `rvMule()`, `ravanaChariot()`, `rvBlade()`;
  `ramSil({ namaste })`, `ramKneel()`; `hut()`, `forestTree()`, `pyre()`; `deviHead({ pal, crown: false })`;
  `screech` and `wingbeat` in `tools/sfx.mjs`; sheet panels 49–61 and `docs/cine_cast_jatayu.jpg`.
- Sound: `tools/jatayu_sfx.mjs` → `assets/jatayu_sfx.wav`, then the mix → `assets/jatayu_mix.wav`: the score at .42 on a
  level curve (.85 under the opening captions, cut to .06 from the sword's stroke at 45.2 to the score's own swell at
  52.75), faded out from 72.4; the SFX at 1.5 on top; loudnorm −15 LUFS.
- The score: `tools/jatayu_score.txt` → `assets/jatayu_score.mp3`, Lyria take 4 of 6 (all six in `out/lyria_jatayu/`),
  used as it came. The brief pinned "96 BPM in 4/4, exactly 30 bars, 75 seconds". Takes 1 and 4 came back at ~76 s,
  2, 3 and 6 stretched to 108–120 s, and 5 returned no audio. Take 4 hits at 15.75, 19.0, 25.25 and 37.5 (after a stop
  at 36.5), hushes at 49.5–52.5, and its choir sings "Jai Shri Ram" at 65.5 and 68.5. TL was laid on those times
  instead of the brief's.
