# "How many times should you walk around HANUMAN?" (ad 65 · Books 1–3 · Pradakshina Part 3 · 3D)

A 66.4 s explainer in the **3D** style, the sequel to ad 50 (Pradakshina Part 2, 1.5M views, 16k follows), answering
its comments: the four gods viewers asked for most. Part 2's dusk courtyard (Shiva's shrine in the middle) with four new
corners: Krishna's shrine (NE), Murugan's (SE), a Navagraha platform (SW: the nine grahas on pedestals in their colours
of cloth, Surya in the middle, in the Agamic layout) and Hanuman's shrine with a saffron flag (NW). Each god's emblem in
lines of light (flute and peacock feather, vel, the nine grahas, gada), gold footprint rings, one a round, with a
climbing chime. The twist is the honest one: only Krishna's number is in a Purana. English VO (Sarvam
`girish_en_devotional`, pace 1.2), karaoke captions timed to the voice, SFX under it (music added on Instagram). The
ad 50 look, no film-look fx and nothing metallic or glossy (owner, 30 Oct: ad 64's Pushpak was too shiny). Card: Books
1–3, set of 3 for ₹500.

## Facts used
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| Krishna: four, the same as Vishnu; "Krishna's four is Part 2's verse, from the Narasimha Purana"; on screen *catasraḥ keśave dadyāt*, "Give four rounds to Keshava" | Hari Bhakti Vilasa 8.394, quoting the Narasimha Purana (*nārasiṁhe*): *ekāṁ caṇḍyāṁ ravau sapta tisro dadyād vināyake / catasraḥ keśave dadyāt śive tv ardhapradakṣiṇām* ([wisdomlib, HBV ch. 8](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hari-bhakti-vilasa-sanskrit-text/d/doc493474.html)); Keshava is a name of Vishnu and of Krishna (the slayer of Keshi) |
| Murugan: six, "from a South Indian verse with no known source" | the count verse *ekaṁ vināyake kuryāt dve sūrye trīṇi śaṅkare…* (Ganesha 1, Surya 2, Shiva 3, Vishnu 4, Devi 5, Skanda 6, ashvattha 7), quoted in South Indian puja guides ([madhwafestivals](https://madhwafestivals.com/2016/09/21/how-to-offer-pradakshina-namaskara/), [rajathathablog](https://rajathathablog.blogspot.com/2016/01/pradakshina-or-circumbulation-how-many.html)), attributed to no text in anything we found. Some lists give Subrahmanya seven ([hindu-blog](https://www.hindu-blog.com/2011/03/number-of-pradakshina-or-parikrama-for.html)): in the IG caption |
| Navagraha: nine; temple custom | nine rounds is the common practice, with no scriptural count found; some do 7 or 11 ([hindu-blog](https://www.hindu-blog.com/2011/03/number-of-pradakshina-or-parikrama-for.html), [hindutone](https://hindutone.com/hinduism/navagraha-nine-planets-hindu-deities-explained/)) |
| Hanuman: three, or eleven in many temples; temple custom | three in popular count lists (no text named); eleven rounds as a vow at Hanuman temples (e.g. the Panchmukhi Hanuman temple, Gorakhpur, [shubhpanchang](https://shubhpanchang.com/temples/shri-panchmukhi-hanuman-temple)); no Purana count found |
| "Only ONE of these numbers is in a Purana" | the above: of the four, only Keshava's four has a Purana verse we can cite. The pinned comment invites a verse for the others |
| "Follow your temple's priest" | advice, not a claim; the same sources say customs vary by temple |

Performance only (no claim): the courtyard and where each shrine stands, the emblems, the nine grahas' layout and
cloth colours (the common Agamic arrangement), the saffron flag, the rings, the chimes, "you asked for four more gods"
(ad 50's comments), Part 4's gods (Shani, Lakshmi, Ayyappa: a promise, sources to check before building).

## Voiceover (tools/rounds3_vo.json; captions are the script)
```
hook     How many times should you walk around HANUMAN?
asked    In PART 2, you asked for FOUR MORE GODS.
krishna  For KRISHNA: FOUR, the same as Vishnu.
murugan  For MURUGAN: SIX.
nava     For the NAVAGRAHA, the nine planets: NINE.
hanuman  And for HANUMAN? THREE. / Or, in many temples, ELEVEN.
twist    But only ONE of these numbers is in a PURANA.
kverse   KRISHNA'S four rounds are Part 2's verse, from the NARASIMHA PURANA.
mverse   MURUGAN'S SIX comes from a South Indian verse / with NO KNOWN SOURCE.
custom   HANUMAN and the NAVAGRAHA? / TEMPLE CUSTOM, handed down.
priest   So follow your temple's PRIEST.
save     SCREENSHOT THIS for your next temple visit.
ask      HANUMAN: THREE or ELEVEN? / Comment what YOUR TEMPLE does!
next     PART 4: Shani, Lakshmi, Ayyappa. / FOLLOW so you don't miss it.
```
Speech-to-text (saaras:v3) on every take: word for word after two fixes ("Krishna's four is" was heard as "Krishna's
forest": now "Krishna's four rounds are"; "Screenshot" as "Scrines": the TTS text says "Screen shot").
