# "Vishnu's 9th avatar: is it BALARAM? Or BUDDHA?" (ad 81 · 3D)

A 69.7 s explainer in the **3D** style (owner, 7 Nov 2026: "a reel on the 9th avatar controversy, Balaram or Buddha…
make it such that people engage… 3D explainer… experiment and make it viral"). Ad 77 (Dashavatar) cut its Balaram/Buddha
debate; this reel is the whole debate, built for comments.

**The experiment:** a family argument as the hook, then a twist most people haven't heard. The fight isn't over one
seat; it's **three names (Krishna, Balaram, Buddha) for two places (8 and 9)**, and every famous list leaves a
different one out. The Mahabharat's own list has neither Balaram nor Buddha, and the Bhagavata counts all three and
says the avatars are countless. No verdict. The ask is an identity call-out ("Which two did YOUR family teach you?
Comment, with your STATE!"): name and place call-outs got the most comments so far, and the state turns the comments
into a map of the divide, which invites replies. There's a screenshot board of the five lists, plus "SAVE this for the
next family debate".

English VO (Sarvam `girish_en_devotional`, pace 1.2), karaoke captions, SFX under it (music added on Instagram), the
Navadurga card (no price). Matte look: no fx bloom, cine.js bloom .12, DOF and a gentle grade.

## Facts used
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| Every list agrees on eight: Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashuram, Ram, Kalki; the fight is over places 8 and 9 | true of every list below (Varaha Purana, Gita Govinda, Desika); the Mahabharat's list differs more (a swan first), and the reel says so on its own beat |
| The Varaha Purana picks Krishna and Buddha (8 Krishna, 9 Buddha) | Varaha Purana 4.2: *matsyaḥ kūrmo varāhaś ca narasiṃho 'tha vāmanaḥ / rāmo rāmaś ca kṛṣṇaś ca buddhaḥ kalkī ca te daśa* (the same line is at Padma 6.229.41 and in the Skanda); the two Ramas are Parashurama and Dasharathi ([wisdomlib Padma text](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/padma-purana-sanskrit/d/doc479749.html), vedapath.app Varaha 4.2) |
| Jaydev's Geet Govind picks Balaram and Buddha (8 Haladhara, 9 Buddha) | Gita Govinda 1, the Dashavatara song (*pralaya payodhi jale*): stanza 8 *keśava dhṛta-haladhara-rūpa*, stanza 9 *keśava dhṛta-buddha-śarīra*, then Kalki |
| For Jaydev, Krishna is the one who takes all ten forms | the refrain of every stanza: "O Keshava (Krishna), who took the form of …, *jaya jagadīśa hare*" |
| In the South, Vedanta Desika's hymn picks Balaram and Krishna, with no Buddha at all | Desika's Dashavatara Stotram (Srirangam, 14th c.): verses 2–11 are Matsya … Rama, Balarama, Krishna, Kalki; the summary verse 12 names the ten; no Buddha ([ramanuja.org](https://www.ramanuja.org/sv/acharyas/desika/stotras/dasavatara-nk.txt), [hindupedia](https://hindupedia.com/en/Dasavathara_Stotram)) |
| The Mahabharat's own list has neither of them; it counts a swan | Mahabharata, Shanti Parva, Ganguli section 340 (the Narayaniya): "Appearing in the forms of a swan, a tortoise, a fish … a boar … Man-lion … dwarf … Rama of Bhrigu's race … Rama, the son of Dasaratha … Krishna the scion of the Sattwata race, and lastly as Kalki." Neither Balarama nor Buddha is in it ([neonvagabond mirror of sacred-texts, m12c039](https://neonvagabond.xyz/mirrors/sacred-texts/hin/m12/m12c039.htm)) |
| The Bhagavata Purana counts all three: Balaram 19th, Krishna 20th, Buddha 21st | Bhagavata 1.3.23: "In the nineteenth and twentieth incarnations, the Lord advented Himself as Lord Balarāma and Lord Kṛṣṇa"; 1.3.24: then, in Kali-yuga, Buddha, son of Anjana, in Gaya (next in the count of 22, so 21st) ([vedabase 1.3.23](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/3/23/), [1.3.24](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/3/24/)) |
| And it says Vishnu's avatars are countless, like streams from an endless lake | Bhagavata 1.3.26: *avatārā hy asaṅkhyeyā*, "the incarnations of the Lord are innumerable, like rivulets flowing from inexhaustible sources of water" ([vedabase](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/3/26/)) |

Not said (kept out on purpose): the Bhagavata's "to delude the envious" gloss on Buddha (1.3.24) and any ranking of the
lists; no verdict. "In the South" is Desika's (a Sri Vaishnava acharya of Kanchi and Srirangam); regional patterns beyond
that (Vithoba in Maharashtra, Jagannath in Odisha) go in the pinned comment, not on screen.

Iconography, no claim: the hall of ten stone seats round a pool; bronze emblems (fish, tortoise, the earth on a tusk, the
split pillar, the parasol and water pot, the axe, the bow, the sword; Krishna's flute and peacock feather, Balaram's
plough, the seated Buddha); the swan on the pool; the outer tier of 22; the streams of light and lamps to the horizon
for "countless".

```

fight     Ask this at home, | and watch a FIGHT break out.
eight     Every list agrees | on EIGHT of the ten.
three     The fight is over TWO places, / and THREE names: | KRISHNA, BALARAM, BUDDHA.
varaha    The VARAHA PURANA | picks KRISHNA and BUDDHA.
jaya      Jaydev's GEET GOVIND | picks BALARAM and BUDDHA. / But where is KRISHNA? / For Jaydev, he is the one | who TAKES all ten forms.

mbh       And the MAHABHARAT'S own list? / NEITHER of them. | It counts a SWAN.
bhag      So who is right? / The BHAGAVATA PURANA | counts all THREE. / Balaram 19th, Krishna 20th, | Buddha 21st.
countless And it says Vishnu's avatars | are COUNTLESS, / like streams | from an endless lake.
save      SAVE this | for the next family debate.
ask       Which two did YOUR family | teach you? / Comment, | with your STATE!
```
Spoken the Hindi way (tts): Balaraam, Jaydev's Geet Govind, Vedaant Deshik, Varaah Puraan, Mahaabhaarat, Bhaagavat
Puraan. Speech-to-text (Sarvam saarika:v2.5) on every take: all right. "Balaram, or Buddha?" first came back as
"Balaram aur Buddha" (Hindi *and*), so the hook became "Is it Balaraam? Or Buddha?"; "And Krishna?" ran into the line
before it ("picks Balaram and Buddha and Krishna") and became "But where is Krishna?".

## Shots (times from the voice: src/scenes/ninth_tl.js)
- **A 0–7.4 hook + fight.** Seat 9 lit in the dark, its gold plaque; over it the plough (left) and the Buddha (right),
  "VISHNU'S 9th AVATAR" above, their names under them. Frame 0 is finished. On FIGHT and BREAK they lunge at each
  other: a bronze clash, sparks, a shake.
- **C –10.6 eight.** A crane up to the whole ring: the eight seats light one by one with their emblems and names; seats
  8 and 9 stay dim with a "?".
- **D –16.4 three.** The two empty seats (8, 9), then the three candidates rise out of the pool, named one by one.
- **E–G the lists.** The two-shot over the pool to seats 8 and 9; each list's card at the top (name, source, "8 · X
  9 · Y", its Devanagari line) as the candidates fly in and out. Varaha: Krishna and Buddha, the plough sinks. Jaydev:
  the plough up to 8, the flute leaves; "where is Krishna?" tilts up to the flute glowing in the sky; at TAKES a beam
  of light falls from it to all ten seats. Desika: the Buddha sinks, the flute comes down to 9, NO BUDDHA.
- **H –39.4 Mahabharat.** The plough sinks on NEITHER, the camera tilts down to the pool and a white swan glides in.
- **I –48.7 Bhagavata.** A gong; a taller outer tier of 22 seats rises behind the ring; Balaram, Krishna and Buddha fly
  to 19, 20 and 21 as they're named.
- **J –54.3 countless.** A crane to the sky: streams of light pour out of the pool between the seats and lamps light to
  the horizon; अवतारा ह्यसङ्ख्येयाः.
- **K –62.7 board + ask.** The three hover over the pool; the board of the five lists; SAVE; the ask.
- **Card –69.7.** The Navadurga card, no price.
