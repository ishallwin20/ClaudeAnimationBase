# Ad 14 — "A DEMON KING proposed to MAA DURGA" (Shumbha, Part 4 of Sher · Books 1–3)

A ~63.3 s captioned reel for Navratri (11–19 Oct 2026), the fourth with Sher (ad 10 Vahana Union → ad 11 Durga vs
Mahishasura → ad 12 Kali's tongue → this). The Devi Mahatmya's last war (ch. 5–10) told as a comedy with a fight in
it: the demon king Shumbha hears about the most beautiful woman in the three worlds and sends her a marriage proposal.
Her answer, "Whoever beats me in battle, I'll marry him", is the hook's payoff; the rest is everyone who tried.

**Kept apart from ads 11 and 12.** Ad 11 was the Mahishasura fight at dusk (cold open + rewind, the gods' gifts); ad 12
was Raktabija at night (the tongue). Here: a rom-com hook (a demon holding a marigold varmala, heart eyes); heaven
STOLEN (Airavata sulking with a tear, a callback to the Bal Hanuman reel's Indra); a gold Himalaya; a proposal
scroll that rolls down the mountain; the one-word kill ("HUM"); the CHAMUNDA name joke; Raktabija only as a 4 s nod;
and the line no reel has done: "I am ALONE in this world. They are ALL ME."

```
Logline: A DEMON KING proposed to MAA DURGA. Shumbha and Nishumbha stole heaven: Indra's throne, Indra's elephant, the
         best of everything. His spies Chanda and Munda saw Maa on the Himalaya: "Boss, you have the best of
         everything… except HER." So he sent a proposal: "Marry Shumbha, or his brother. Your choice." Maa: "Sure.
         One tiny condition: whoever beats me in BATTLE, I'll marry him." Then everyone tried: Dhumralochana and
         60,000 demons (one word, "HUM", and he's ash; Sher takes the 60,000), Chanda and Munda (Kali bursts out of
         her forehead: she's named CHAMUNDA), Raktabija, Nishumbha. Shumbha, alone: "You CHEAT! You win with HELP!"
         Maa: "I am ALONE in this world. They are ALL ME." Every shakti flies back into her. One on one, she won.
         The garland lands on Sher.
Why it should travel: a proposal is a joke everyone gets, and "her answer was LEGENDARY" holds people to the reveal; the
         condition line is quotable (and very Navratri); "HUM" is a two-second wow; CHAMUNDA = CHAnda + MUNDA is a

         the battlefield at dusk (the fights: ad 11's set) → a red sky (Shumbha alone), blazing gold (ALL ME) →
         brand peach (card).
```

## Facts used (Devi Mahatmya / Durga Saptashati, ch. 5–10)
- Shumbha and Nishumbha, asura brothers, took Indra's sovereignty and the shares of the other gods (ch. 5).
- Chanda and Munda, Shumbha's servants, saw Ambika on the Himalaya and told him: you have Airavata, the Parijata tree,
  Uchchaihshravas and every jewel of the three worlds; take this jewel among women too (ch. 5).
- Shumbha sent the messenger Sugriva: accept me or my brother Nishumbha. The Devi: what you say is true, but in my
  folly I made a vow: whoever conquers me in battle, removes my pride and is my match in strength, he shall be my
  husband (ch. 5).
- Dhumralochana came with 60,000 asuras; the Devi reduced him to ashes with the sound "hum", and her lion destroyed the
  army (ch. 6).
- Against Chanda and Munda, the Devi's face went dark with anger and Kali sprang from her forehead; she killed them,
  and the Devi named her Chamunda (ch. 7).
- Raktabija (ch. 8, ad 12), then Nishumbha (ch. 9).
- Shumbha: you are proud, but you fight relying on the strength of others. The Devi: "I am alone here in the world.
  Who else is there besides me? See, these are my own powers entering into me." The goddesses merged into her, and
  she fought him alone and slew him (ch. 10). (Retold for kids: he is launched into the sky.)
