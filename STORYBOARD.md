# Ad 52: "The gold thread" (Books 1–3)

**Style:** Cinematic engine (`src/cine.js`), a new abstract look: lives as threads of light on a night sky. Inspired
by a "life flow" reel the owner liked (threads rising up an age axis, the ones who die become stars, a family
constellation at the end), turned into our own story: **the stories are a gold thread passed down the family.**
No voice; Lyria score (piano, bansuri, strings) + a soft SFX layer (`tools/katha_thread_sfx.mjs`). 70 s.

**Hook / ask:** "Your life, as a thread. Watch the gold one." → "Who told *you* your first story? Comment their name."
(name call-outs are what gets the most comments.) **Card:** `rkCardCine`, "Pass the stories on", Books 1–3 set ₹500.

## How it works
- Every person is a glowing thread. The "now" line sits at y 1060; the past scrolls down beneath it. The age axis on the
  left is Me's age, and time speeds up and slows down (`AGEK`).
- Nani's thread has a gold core from the start. Each story she tells is a gold arc from her thread to Me's, with the
  story's name on it (Ganesha, Hanuman, Little Krishna, Prahlad, Dhruva), and Me's thread turns gold.
- When a thread ends, its light rises into the sky as a star with the person's name.
- Me tells the stories to the little one, then to the grandkid. Me becomes a star; the stars join into a constellation;
  the gold line runs Nani → Me → the little one, and the little one goes on telling them to the grandkid.
