# Ad 18: "VISHNU faked a SUNSET." (the Gokarna Atmalinga, as the sources tell it · Books 1–3)

A ~60 s captioned Painted reel: no voiceover, synthesized SFX only, captions carry the story. This redoes ad 17, which
was deleted because its premise ("Ravana needed a toilet") came from a folk variant. In the sourced telling, Ravana
stops for his **evening prayers** because Vishnu hides the sun to fake a dusk. Every plot beat below is in "Facts used",
with sources (the CLAUDE.md rule: retellings stay true to a source). The comedy lives in the performance: the ten heads,
the disguise, the timing.

```
Logline: Shiva gives Ravana his Atmalinga: wherever it is first set on the ground, it stays. The gods panic, so Vishnu
         hides the sun to fake an early dusk. Ravana never misses his evening prayers, but he can't pray holding it and
         can't put it down. A little brahmin boy (totally NOT Ganesha) holds it, calls him three times VERY fast while
         he prays, and sets it down. It stays forever at Gokarna; Ravana's pull leaves it shaped like a cow's ear; he
         strikes the boy, and the Ganesha there still has the dent.
Hook:    Frame 0: Ravana hugging the glowing linga, ten heads staring up at the sun as a spinning chakra slides over it
         (Vishnu's blue finger poking up from a cloud). Caption already up: "VISHNU faked" / "a SUNSET." (gold).
World:   snowy Kailash → Indra's cloud → a hot noon road that turns to a fake orange dusk → the Gokarna coast → the
         temple sanctum → a real sunset for the ask → the brand peach card.
Motifs:  the WAVE (ten heads react left→right), THREE (the one rule, the three calls, Shiva counting along), the DENT
         (the bump → a match cut to the idol's dent).
```

## Things to notice (the rewatch layer; none claims a plot fact)
1. **Vishnu's finger**: the chakra spins on a blue fingertip poking out of a cloud.
2. **The noon shadow**: during the "sunset", Ravana's shadow is still a short noon blob right under his feet.
3. **SLEEPY sleeps through it all**, even the prayers; the BONK finally wakes him.
4. **The disguise**: a fake moustache just like Ravana's; it slips, he pushes it back with his trunk. Mooshak peeks out
   of the kamandalu.
5. **SIDE-EYE knows**: he narrows his eyes at the trunk, and opens one eye mid-prayer when the calls start.
6. **Shiva on a cloud** counts the three calls on his fingers, and winces at the bonk.
7. **The cow** flicks its ear when the linga takes the shape of a cow's ear.
