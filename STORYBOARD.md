# Ramayan · Part 8: "The Arrow from Behind a Tree" (ad 40 · the Ramayan series)

Part 8 of the 12-part Ramayan series (format in ad 33's storyboard). Painted, **no voice** (owner, 23 Oct: the chibi
reels without a VO do best): Bangers captions carry the story (timeline and captions in `src/scenes/rp8_tl.js`), the
characters talk with moving mouths, and a retro cartoon SFX track sits under it (about -16 LUFS, fixed gain); the only
music is a soft tanpura and veena under the anklets and Vali's last words. 67.2 s. Made extra cute (owner, 23 Oct:
"extremely cute, viral"): Hanuman the shy monk, Lakshman blushing with his eyes shut over the earrings, the anklet's
bells, Sugriva flung out with tweeting stars, Ram's puzzled "?"s at the look-alike brothers, the garland's sparkle. The
hook and the ask: Ram shot Vali from behind a tree; was it fair? Comment YES or NO (the card asks it too).

Not claimed: Ram's further arguments in sarga 18 (Vali as a vanara, the hunter's right), left out for time; Tara's
lament (sarga 19–20). "Couldn't shoot" stands for Ram's own words that he could not tell them apart (sarga 12). The
"was it fair?" question is the reel's, not the text's. Performance only: the "?" by the tree, Hanuman's blush and the
notes, Lakshman's blush and sweat drop, the anklet's sparkle, the stars over Sugriva, the dust cloud, the garland's
sparkle, the scales with YES and NO.
