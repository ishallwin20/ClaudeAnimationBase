# Shiv Tandav (Cel style, 16 s loop)

Chibi Shiva dances on the snows of Kailash to the Shiv Tandav clip from the owner's Instagram example (StudioLKY's
chibi Shiva Tandav), which the owner chose as the reel's song. The first reel in the Cel style: a cel-animated
character (flat fills, a cel shade, a navy ink line that boils) held on threes over a Painterly snowfield.

- Song: `assets/shiva_tandav_song.wav`, cut from the example (15.9 s). Kept out of git. 150 BPM, a beat every 0.400 s
  from t = 0 (onsets at 0.80, 1.21, 1.61, 2.41, 4.01, 4.41, 4.81 … 15.59, all on the grid). Whisper could not
  transcribe the chant, so no lyric lines are shown: only the title.
- The example's dancer draws a new picture every 0.1 s and changes pose about every 0.8 s, so: a key pose every two
  beats (20 a loop), a bounce on the beat between, each move 0.45 s with an overshoot as it lands.

## Facts used
- The Shiva Tandava Stotram names its singer in its closing verse: "पूजावसानसमये दशवक्त्रगीतं …" (sung by
  Dashavaktra, the ten-faced one, i.e. Ravana).
- Ravana lifts Kailash, Shiva presses it down with his toe and pins his arms, Ravana praises Shiva for a long time,
  Shiva frees him and names him Ravana for the cry that made the worlds shake: Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda,
  sarga 16. That the hymn he sang was this stotram is tradition, and the caption says so.
- Shiva's marks as drawn: the crescent moon, the Ganga in his jata, Vasuki round his neck, the tripundra and the
  third eye, the damaru and trishul, the Nataraja pose (Ananda Tandava). The dance itself is a performance, not a
  story beat.

# Rudra Tandav (the fierce version, `rudra.html`)

The same song, beat map and dance as "Shiv Tandav" (the same 20 slots, with the rage pose and more stomps), every
move bigger (lean and head tilt × 1.4, faster moves with more overshoot), in a storm.

- Set: `celStormfield`: a violet-black sky burning red and orange at the horizon, storm clouds lit from below, the
  peak of Kailash dark against the fire, dark snow catching red light, black rocks with red edges.
- Shiva: `fierce` (no blush, brows drawn down, eyes shut tight, a grim mouth; a roar with teeth on the squats and the
  rage), six jata lashing out on both sides, a deeper blue; fire on his edges and underside, the lightning on his
  top-left edge. The third eye opens and burns on the stomps (0, 4.0, 12.0 s), the rage (9.6) and the Nataraja (15.2).
- Lightning: twin bolts on frame 0 and on the rage, strikes into the trishul on the damaru (1.6) and the Nataraja
  (15.2), bolts on the stomps and at 6.4, 8.0, 13.6; flickers at 1.2, 7.6, 10.8, 13.0, 14.4.
- The ring of fire (the Nataraja's prabha) behind him, leaping on each landing; fire cracks and a shock ring on the
  stomps, which also shake the frame; sparks where a foot lands; embers rising.
- Title: HAR HAR MAHADEV / रुद्र ताण्डव. Cover: the rage under the twin bolts at 9.65 s (`rudra_cover.html`).

## Facts used (Rudra Tandav)
- Shiva as Nataraja, the lord of dance; the tandava's two moods: Ananda Tandava (bliss) and Rudra Tandava (fierce,
  destruction, the dance at the end of a world): the standard classification in Shaiva tradition and iconography.
- His fiercest tandava after Sati's self-immolation at Daksha's yagna: tradition (the Sati story in the Shiva and
  Devi Bhagavata Puranas; the caption says "tradition remembers").
