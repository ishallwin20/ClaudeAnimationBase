# "TULSI VIVAH is on SATURDAY. SAVE this: the whole wedding, step by step." (ad 105 · 3D)

A 98.7 s save-bait how-to in the **3D** style, made on 20 Nov 2026 (Prabodhini Ekadashi) for Tulsi Vivah on Sat 21 Nov.
Owner: "Lets make a 3D explainer on how to do tulasi vivah puja correctly. Use sources and create a beautiful 3D explainer
reel." The format is ad 83 (Lakshmi Puja step by step): a numbered checklist with a big STEP N in the top band, Girish VO
(pace 1.25), a save prompt in the hook and at the end, the Navadurga card (no price). It follows ad 104 (Painterly, *why*
we marry Tulsi to a stone), so this is the *how*.

**The idea:** one set, filmed like a wedding. A home courtyard at godhuli: the tulsi chaura in lime and geru, the tulsi
growing in it, a rangoli round it. Step by step she becomes a bride under a sugarcane mandap, the Shaligram is bathed,
dressed and seated beside her, they are given, tied, showered with rice and kumkum, and the Shaligram is carried round
her while the light goes from golden to blue hour and the Dwadashi moon comes up. The ask is a real household difference:
**how many rounds in your home?** (guides give seven, some four; some homes do none).
