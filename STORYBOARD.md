# Pass it on (reel/diya-pass-it-on)

A 20 s Diwali loop in the **Cel** style (flat fills, cel shade, boiling navy ink, paper grain), drawn on twos at 12
drawings a second and rendered at 24 fps. The owner asked for one diya lighting the others with a magical spark, after an
Instagram example (a wall of four diyas lit by a wisp). The technique is the same, but the setting, camera and diya
design are ours, and the reel tells a different story: home finds you. No card. The Rishi Katha watermark (logo +
@therishikatha) is on every frame, low in the safe band. Posted on Lakshmi Puja day, 8 Nov 2026.

| t (s) | shot | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4.0 | village courtyard | Her diya burns on the tulsi chaura and she stands in the warm doorway under a mango-leaf toran. Hook: "Far from home / this Diwali?" Three sparks lift off the flame (0.9), the camera tilts up past the roof, the neem and the palm, and the sparks become a wisp. |
| 4.0–8.4 | the journey | The camera pans across the night: huts with lit doorways, fields, a pond full of stars, a small temple, a train on a river bridge, the city with its far crackers. The wisp leads and dives into a tower. |
| 8.4–13.7 | city balcony | Four unlit diyas wait on a parapet; inside, a young one sits on the sofa looking at a phone. The wisp lights them left to right (9.6, 10.35, 11.1, 11.85) and the young one looks up (9.9). Caption: "Her diya still finds you." |
| 13.1–17.0 | the burst | A spark rises from the third diya, the camera tilts up past the roof and the water tank, and it bursts (15.35): "Happy Diwali". One ember falls out of frame. |
| 17.0–20.0 | home | The ember falls back into the village sky, the camera tilts down with it, and it lands in her diya (18.85), which flares. The hook fades back up (19.2) and the loop closes on frame 0. |

Sound: `tools/diya_home_sfx.mjs` → `assets/diya_home_sfx.wav` (crickets, a shimmer, air, a far train and crackers,
a chime a step higher for each diya, a whistle, the burst, a low bell at the landing). Music is added on Instagram.

## Facts used
Not a retelling: no plot facts. The details are true to the festival. Diwali falls on the Kartik amavasya, so there is
no moon in any sky. Diyas are clay lamps. The tulsi in a whitewashed chaura is lit at dusk in many homes, and the toran
of mango leaves hangs over the door.
