# "Is HANUMAN alive?" (ad 84 · no-price card)

An 82.5 s reel for the main followers: how powerful Hanuman was, told through the one line that says it best. When
the whole army lay fallen, the oldest of them asked only whether Hanuman lived. **3D** (the Himalaya at night, the map
of India) with **Cinematic** silhouettes (the battlefield, Jambavan, Hanuman grown huge), the film look, karaoke
captions, no voice; SFX and a Lyria score.

Why this and not ad 32 again: ad 32 (Painted) told the Sanjeevani night of Yuddha 101 (Lakshman and the spear, "his
second mountain"). This is the first mountain, Yuddha 74: the whole army down, Ram too, and Jambavan's question.

## Facts used (Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 74; K. M. K. Murthy's translation, [valmikiramayan.net prose](https://www.sanskritdocuments.org/sites/valmikiramayan/yuddha/sarga74/yuddha_74_prose.htm))
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| One Brahmastra felled 67 crore warriors | "Sixty seven crores of powerful monkeys were struck down by the cherished missile of Brahma … in the fifth and last period of the day" (Indrajit's, from sarga 73) |
| Even Ram and Lakshman lay senseless | "Rama and Lakshmana were knocked down senseless" |
| Two figures walk the field with torches | "Hanuma and Vibhishana, together at that time of the night, roamed about in the battle-front, with torches in their hands" |
| Old Jambavan, blind with arrows | "Pierced with sharp arrows, I am not able to see you with my eyes" |
| He asked only one thing: is Hanuman alive? | "Does Hanuma, the foremost among monkeys … [hold] his life any where nearby?" |
| "Why not ask for Ram?" | Vibhishana: "why do you enquire about Hanuman, passing over the venerable sons of Dasaratha?" |
| "If Hanuman lives, we all live" | "If that brave Hanuma is surviving, the whole army even if killed, is unhurt … if Hanuma has given up his life, we are all dead, even though living" |
| Hanuman at his feet | "offered his salutation, by grasping the feet of Jambavan" |
| "Go to the Himalaya. Bring the herbs." | "You ought to go to Himalaya … by traveling higher and higher over the large ocean"; four herbs named (Mrita Sanjivani, Vishalyakarani, Suvarnakarani, Sandhani) |
| He stood on Trikuta like a second mountain; it sank; Lanka shook | "pressing the foremost of mountains (with his feet), that valiant Hanuma appeared like a second mountain"; the mountain "was not able to bear its weight and sank"; Lanka "as though dancing at night" |
| The mountain of herbs between Kailasa and Rishabha, blazing | "you will see there the Mount Rishabha … and the very much powerful peak of Kailasa … In the midst of these two peaks, you will see a blazing and unequally brilliant herbal mountain"; "shining with a heap of fire" |
| The herbs saw him coming and hid | "Knowing then that somebody was coming in search of them, all the distinguished herbs on that mountain disappeared from Hanuma's view" |
| "No mercy for Ram? Then he'd take it all" (a paraphrase, no quote marks) | "Is it quite sure that you have not shown any compassion for Rama? If it is so, overcome by the strength of my arms; find yourself shattered to pieces today" |
| He tore out the whole peak | "Grasping that mountain-peak, radiant as the sun … Having uprooted that mountain" |
| He crossed the night like a second sun | "having reached the orbit of the sun, shone as a second sun" |
| One breath of its fragrance and they all rose, as if from sleep | "By inhaling that fragrance of those great herbs, both Rama and Lakshamana became healed"; the monkeys rose "even as those who are asleep fully get up at the close of night" |

Not claimed: a deadline of dawn, Sushena, Kalanemi, Bharat's arrow (Manas, or sarga 101); how big the peak was; the
distance in km. In the caption only: he carried the mountain back ("carried away that mountain of herbs quickly back
to the mountains of Himalayas"). Lanka and the herb mountain are placed on today's map by tradition (Sri Lanka; the
Kailash region), as in ads 42 and 64. Performance only: Hanuman grown to a giant behind the peak (the text has him
grow his body on Mount Malaya and seize the peak with his arms), his burning eyes, the moon, the rocks falling, the
closing picture of Hanuman kneeling to Ram, and the closing line ("Is Hanuman with you?").

## Captions (the whole script)
```
67 crore warriors. One Brahmastra.              Even Ram and Lakshman lay senseless.
Old Jambavan, blind with arrows,                asked only one thing:
Is HANUMAN alive?                               Why not ask for Ram?
"If Hanuman lives, we ALL live."                "Go to the Himalaya. Bring the herbs."
From Lanka to the Himalaya.                     A mountain blazing with healing herbs.
But the herbs saw him… and hid.                 No mercy for Ram? Then he'd take it ALL.
He tore out the whole mountain.                 He crossed the night like a second sun.
One breath of its fragrance…                    and they all rose, as if from sleep.
When all seems lost, ask only one thing:        Is Hanuman with you?
Comment JAI HANUMAN
```
