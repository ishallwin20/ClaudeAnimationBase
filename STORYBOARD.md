## Facts used

- Bhagavata Purana 10.8.32: Balarama and the other cowherd boys tell Yashoda, "Krishna has eaten mud."
- 10.8.35: Krishna: "Mother, I have not eaten mud; they are all lying. If you think they speak the truth, look into my
  mouth yourself" (nāhaṁ bhakṣitavān amba sarve mithyābhiśaṁsinaḥ).
- 10.8.36: Yashoda: "If so, then open" (yady evaṁ tarhi vyādehi); he opens his mouth.
- 10.8.37–39: inside she sees the whole universe (moving and unmoving beings, space, the directions, mountains, islands,
  oceans, the earth, wind, fire, the moon and stars, the planets), and Vraja along with herself (vrajaṁ sahātmānam).
- 10.8.43–44: Yogamaya makes her forget, and she takes him on her lap with more love than before.
- Radha (as in #1): Brahmavaivarta Purana, Garga Samhita, the Gita Govinda; not named in the Bhagavata. In the lila it
  is Yashoda who looks; here Radha takes her part, and the caption says so.
- What the reel invents (performance only, no plot claim): Radha being there, the floating, the tiny Radha waving, the
  clod drifting past, the gulp, "That's a planet", the giggle. The mud on his face follows from 10.8.32.
