# Saanwaro nahi aayo (Mira's song), a follower collab

A poem in Mira's voice, written and sung by **Shivangi Singh (@thebrowngirl_ss)**, a follower. Style: **Miniature**
(new, Nov 2026; MINIATURE_GUIDE.md). 127.5 s, 9:16. Her reverb take (assets/vo/mira/shivangi_1_reverb.mp3, out of git)
carries the reel; the verse is written on the inscription band as she sings it, Devanagari with the English meaning.
Her recording sings verses 1, 2 and 4 (the "dhruv tara" verse is not sung): the reel follows the recording and the
caption carries the whole poem. Krishna never appears: the poem says he did not come. No book card (collab): the card
is "follow both".

Not a retelling: no purana plot is claimed. Mira's iconography only (white odhni, ektara, Giridhar Gopal's murti,
dancing in ghungroo: "pag ghunghroo bandh Mira nachi re" is her own pad).

Sound (tools/mira_sfx.mjs): a tanpura on her Sa (~250 Hz, from her phrase endings); a bansuri in Kafi answering each line
in the pauses (his flute, heard, never seen); the bahroopiya's flute under "Mohan roop", cut when he lowers it; ektara
in the shrine; ghungroo on her steps; parrot wings; crickets and far thunder; the river, rain, the sea; a chime as the
lamp touches the water. Music ducked under her voice, -14 LUFS.

English meanings to confirm with the poet: line 2 (पलकन छांवो उतारौ: "spread my lashes as shade on his path") and
line 11 (तोहरे मुख की लहरी देखे: "since I saw the play of light on your face").
