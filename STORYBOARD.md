# Ramayan · Part 10: "Lanka Burns" (ad 45 · the Ramayan series)

Part 10 of the 12-part Ramayan series (format in ad 33's storyboard). Painted, **no voice**, like Parts 2–9: Bangers
captions carry the story (timeline and captions in `src/scenes/rp10_tl.js`), the characters talk with moving mouths,
retro cartoon SFX under it (about -16 LUFS, fixed gain); the only music is a soft tanpura under Sita's prayer and her
jewel. About 70 s. Made very cute: Hanuman's lightbulb grin before he wrecks the grove (Sita's tree left standing with a
"ting"), soldiers bonked into tweeting stars, his wink as the Brahmastra's glowing ropes wrap him, Ravana's ten heads
each losing it in their own way, the fire on his tail that only makes him go "ahh", roof-to-roof hops with the city
going up behind, the hiss and the steam cloud when he dips his tail in the sea, and the panic: "Is Sita safe?!"

The hook: Ravana set Hanuman's tail on fire… BIG MISTAKE (frame 0: Hanuman grinning on a rooftop at night, his tail
ablaze). The ask on the card: "Comment JAI BAJRANGBALI 🙏". The books line: "Our picture books · rishikatha.com".

## Facts used (Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda; valmikiramayan.net numbering)

|---|---|

| He leaves alone the tree where Sita sits (the shimshapa) | Sundara 41–42 |

| Indrajit binds Hanuman with the Brahmastra; Hanuman lets himself be taken, wanting to speak with Ravana | Sundara 48 |
| In Ravana's court, Hanuman tells him to give Sita back | Sundara 51 |

| They wrap his tail in cloth soaked in oil and light it | Sundara 53 |
| Sita prays to Agni to be cool for Hanuman, and the fire doesn't hurt him | Sundara 53 |
| He slips his bonds, leaps from roof to roof and sets Lanka on fire | Sundara 53–54 |
| He spares Vibhishana's house | Sundara 54 |
| He puts out his tail in the sea | Sundara 54 |
| He panics that Sita may have burned, then finds her safe | Sundara 55–56 |
| The Part 11 tease: Ram and the ocean | Yuddha 21–22 (Part 11) |

Not claimed (folk, not Valmiki): Hanuman coiling his tail into a seat higher than Ravana's throne; the tail growing
longer as they ran out of cloth. Left out for time: the soldiers' numbers, Jambumali, the ministers' sons and the five
generals (42–46), Hanuman's long speech (51). "Defeated" stands for Akshaya's death in the text (kept gentle). Performance
only: the lightbulb, the "ting" for Sita's tree, the stars and tweets, the wink, the ten heads' reactions, the "ahh",
the steam cloud, Sita waving.
