# Ad 100: "There is ONE weapon you beat by DROPPING yours." (the Narayanastra)

3D style (a cosmic Kurukshetra) with the comic's cast drawn in 2D over it, Girish VO, SFX-led mix (−14 LUFS), ~92 s.
First reel of the Divyastra idea (owner, 17 Nov 2026: "3D explainer series on Divyaastras… make it extremely viral…
insert advertisement of Ashwatthama Comic"). It stands alone (no "Part 1"): series decay is the reason (Hanuman P1–2
250k, P7–9 ~45k). End card: the Ashwatthama comic (no price), led in by four of its real pages and its cover.

**Why it should travel:** the hook is a paradox (you win by dropping your weapon), the rule is spoken twice
("even in his mind"), the twist lands late (Narayana's weapon stopped by Narayana), and the ask is a fight
("Bhima: brave or foolish?"). The reel ends on Ashwatthama, so the comic is the answer to "what happened next".

## Facts used (Mahabharata, critical edition, Drona Parva; GRETIL mbh_07)
- Drona killed after the "Ashwatthama is dead" ruse (an elephant of that name), having laid down his weapons: 7.164.68–109, 7.166.16, 7.166.19 ("my father, his weapons laid down").
- Ashwatthama's tears and rage: 7.166.16–18.
- Drona got the Narayana weapon from Narayana himself, after bowing to him; Narayana warned it must never be used in haste, does not return without killing, and kills even those who should not be killed; ways to calm it include laying down weapons and seeking refuge: 7.166.43–51.
- On its release: wind and rain from a cloudless sky, the earth shook, the ocean churned, rivers flowing to the sea flowed backwards, mountain peaks split, the sun darkened: 7.167.1–4.
- Thousands of blazing arrows like fire-mouthed snakes, glowing iron balls, shataghnis, razor-edged discs: 7.170.16–19.
- The more the Pandavas fought, the more the weapon grew: 7.170.21.
- Krishna stopped the army with his arms: drop your weapons, get down from your vehicles to the ground; those who lay down weapons it will not kill; whoever fights it even in his mind it will kill, even in Rasatala: 7.170.37–42.
- Bhima refused: "I will stop it…", "I alone among men have the strength of ten thousand elephants": 7.170.44–51 (nāgāyutasamaprāṇa, 7.170.49).
- Everyone else dropped their weapons and came down; the weapon's force fell on Bhima's head: 7.170.58–60.
- Arjuna covered Bhima with the Varuna weapon; Krishna and Arjuna jumped down, ran into the fire, unarmed, and it did not burn them; Nara and Narayana dragged Bhima and his weapons away: 7.171.1–13.
- Krishna threw him to the ground; when his weapon was on the ground the Narayanastra was calmed; the sky cleared: 7.171.15–22.
- Duryodhana asked for it again; "it does not return, it cannot be used twice; called back, it kills its user": 7.171.24–27.
- The night raid ("three days later"): Drona falls on day 15, the Sauptika raid is the night after day 18 (the comic, Sauptika Parva).

**Kept out:** "ancient nuclear weapon" claims; any power ranking of astras; the Varuna weapon (true, but a beat too many);
Yudhishthira's despair (7.170.24–36); that the fire spared Krishna and Arjuna partly because of the Varuna weapon
(the reel says only that they ran in unarmed).
