# Ad 20: "RAVANA lifted KAILASH. His whole court couldn't move ONE monkey's FOOT." (Cinematic · Books 1–3 · 75.4 s)

A captioned retelling in the **Cinematic** style ([CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md](CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md)) of the Angad–Ravan samvad,
as Goswami Tulsidas tells it in the Ramcharitmanas (Lanka Kand). There is no voice. The captions tell it, synthesized SFX
carry the hits, and a Lyria 3 Pro score sits underneath. It ends on the Books 1–3 card (set of 3 for ₹500) with the
Follow line. It is meant to post for Dussehra (20 Oct 2026), when the scene is staged at every Ramlila.

```
Logline: Ram sends Angad, Vali's son, to Lanka to offer peace. The whole court stands when he walks in. "Who are you,
         monkey?" "Angad, son of your friend Vali." "And how is my friend?" "Go and ask him, in ten days." Angad roasts
         him: of all the Ravanas he has heard of, one was caught by Sahasrabahu and one lived under Vali's arm, so which
         one is this? Ravana boasts of Kailash and of the heads he offered Shiva. Angad strikes the ground; Ravana's
         crowns fall; he flings them across the sea, and Hanuman catches them for Ram. Then he plants his foot: "Move it,
         and Ram turns back; Sita stays here." Warriors as strong as Meghnad heave at it; it doesn't move; they sit back
         down, heads hung. Ravana comes down himself and bends to grab it. Angad pulls it away: "Why grab MY feet? Fall
         at RAM's feet." Ravana sits, head bowed, his glory gone. Angad returns and falls at Ram's feet. JAI SHRI RAM.
Why it should travel: the most famous Ravana image after Kailash (ad 15, 1M views), a physical challenge anyone can
         picture, a roast played on ten faces (comedy in the performance, every line sourced), a payoff line that is the
         moral, Dussehra timing, and a name call-out (the best comment lever).

```

## Facts used
Every plot beat is from the Ramcharitmanas, Lanka Kand (Gita Press numbering; text checked on ramcharit.in). The foot
challenge is Tulsidas's: in the Valmiki Ramayana (Yuddha Kanda 41) Angad delivers Ram's message, shakes off the four
rakshasas sent to seize him and breaks the palace roof with his foot. The caption names the source and says so.

Not claimed: how many crowns fell (the text gives some to Ravana's own hands and flings the rest; the reel says
"crowns"); "six months" under Vali's arm (not in this passage); Ravana's son killed on the way in (skipped); that Ravana
himself failed to move the foot (he reached for it and Angad pulled it away, so the hook says "his whole court"). The
tug-of-war chain, the hands in the hook and the kicking legs under Vali's arm are how the reel stages "rushed in and
couldn't move it" and "held in Vali's armpit"; they claim no extra plot.
