## Facts used
- Kushmanda is the fourth of the nine forms of Durga (Navadurga): Devi Kavacham, recited with the Durga Saptashati
  ("prathamam shailaputri cha … kushmandeti chaturthakam").
- Before creation there was only darkness; the Devi as Kushmanda smiled and her smile brought the universe (the
  cosmic egg, brahmanda) into being; she is called the Adi Shakti of creation for it. The standard Navadurga account
  of Kushmanda, as retold in the Navaratri day-4 traditions (e.g. Drik Panchang, "Maa Kushmanda"; Rishi Katha Book 4).
- The name read as ku (a little) + ushma (warmth, energy) + anda (the cosmic egg): the commonly given meaning in the
  same accounts.
- She dwells in the core of the Sun (the Surya mandala) and gives it its light and energy: same accounts.
- Her form: eight arms (Ashtabhuja Devi) holding a kamandalu, bow, arrow, lotus, a pot of amrit, chakra, mace and a
  japa mala; she rides a lion. (The generated painting was checked for all eight.)
- No comedy, no invented beats: the picture adds only light (the spark, the egg, the burst, the sun) to the words.
