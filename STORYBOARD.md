# Ram meets all of Ayodhya: "Amit roop pragate" (Painted, collab)

A collab reel with **@lame_batman (Suryakant Sharma)**: his Hindi voiceover (69 s, `assets/vo/ramji/`, cleaned to
`clean.wav`, kept out of git), our picture. Painted style (the Ramayan series cast), Bangers subtitles that are the VO
transliterated into Roman Hindi (not translated), names in gold, the two chaupais also in Devanagari. Ends on a
"follow both" card. ~71 s.

Logline: Ram comes home after fourteen years; all Ayodhya rushes to meet him, a little boy at the back can't even see
him, so Ram appears in countless forms and meets every single person at once.

World: dawn sky → the meadow outside Ayodhya's gold walls by the Saryu, festive (torans, flags, petals), warm morning
light warming to gold. Motif: the little boy in a turquoise dhoti who can't see (set up in G, paid off in L, in the
last wide). Rhyme: open on one Ram on the Pushpak, end on one Ram among everyone.

## Facts used

- Ram returns to Ayodhya on the Pushpak with Sita, Lakshman and the vanara host (Hanuman among them) after the victory
  over Ravana, the fourteen years complete: Valmiki Yuddha Kanda 121–127 (Vibhishana gives the Pushpak, the vanaras
  board, the flight, the arrival at Nandigram); Ramcharitmanas Lanka Kanda doha 118–121 and Uttar Kanda doha 1–4.
- The city waited for this day; when the vimana came down, the people rushed out to meet him: Ramcharitmanas Uttar
  Kanda doha 3–5.
- "प्रभु बिलोकि हरषे पुरबासी। जनित बियोग बिपति सब नासी॥": seeing the Lord, the townspeople rejoiced; every sorrow born
  of the separation was gone. Ramcharitmanas, Uttar Kanda, after doha 5.
- "प्रेमातुर सब लोग निहारी। कौतुक कीन्ह कृपाल खरारी॥ अमित रूप प्रगटे तेहि काला। जथा जोग मिले सबहि कृपाला॥": seeing
  everyone restless with love, the Lord appeared at that moment in countless forms and met each one as was fitting;
  "छन महिं सबहि मिले भगवाना। उमा मरम यह काहुँ न जाना॥": in an instant he met them all, and no one knew the secret.
  Ramcharitmanas, Uttar Kanda, doha 6 (chaupais before it).
- He first met the gurus and Bharata, then the people (Uttar Kanda 5–6): the queens and Bharata are shown with him.
- On screen nothing claims the lamps of Diwali (not in this passage); Ayodhya is decorated with torans, flags and
  kalash, and flowers rain (Uttar Kanda 3–4, "सुमन बृष्टि").

## Sound

The cleaned VO on top. A mild synth bed (tanpura, a soft pad), never loud, side-chain ducked under the voice. Quiet
SFX: wind and a whoosh in the sky, temple bells and a shankh at the landing, crowd murmur and a cheer, poofs and a
shimmer as the forms appear, a soft chime as each sorrow-cloud pops, bells on "Jai Shri Ram", the card chime.
