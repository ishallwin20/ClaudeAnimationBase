# "How many KRISHNAS?" (trial reel, Painted)

A find-them puzzle in the duck-reel format (`~/Downloads/ig_find_duck.mp4`): one finished picture that loops for 8 s,
a counting question, and no reveal in the video. The answer goes in the pinned comment after a day. It's a trial reel
for followers: no end card (owner, 25 Oct 2026). It also sets up Little Kanha Part 4 (Brahma hides the calves and boys),
which the Part 3 card promised.

**Logline:** a picnic on the Yamuna sand: Kanha in the middle, his friends round him, the calves grazing. Only one of
them looks like Krishna. Every one of them is.

**World:** Vrindavan on a warm late afternoon. The kadamb tree with its gold ball flowers, Govardhan on the horizon,
the soft sand bank (pulina) of the dark-blue Yamuna, lotus leaves on the water. Palette: honey sky, sap greens, sand,
Yamuna indigo-teal, Kanha's blue and yellow as the only blue-and-yellow pair in the frame (until you look at the water).

**Frame (1080 × 1920, a still camera; everything that tells the puzzle sits in y 420–1500, x < 930):**
- 440–640: the question, "How many KRISHNAS?" (big), "Even BRAHMA got this wrong" (gold).
- 650–900: sky, a cloud with Brahma peeking over it (small, three of his four faces), Govardhan, the kadamb tree.
- 820–1050: the meadow: cows and calves grazing, small and far.
- 1000–1260: the sand bank: Kanha sitting in the middle eating, nine gopas round him, calves at the flanks.
- 1260–1920: the Yamuna. Two boys and a drinking calf stand at the edge; their reflections are blue Kanhas.
- 1460: "Follow if you found them ALL".

**Loop (8 s = 12 beats at 90 bpm; every motion has a period of 8, 4 or 2 s, so the seam can't be seen):**
calves graze and lift their heads, tails swish, one calf frisks (a hop) once a loop; Kanha brings a morsel to his mouth
and chews every 4 s; a gopa tosses a laddoo up and catches it; another laughs; feathers wobble; bells sway; the river
ripples and the reflections waver; Brahma rises out of his cloud, stares, and sinks back once a loop.

**Clues, easy to hard:**
1. Kanha himself (the obvious "1").
2. The reflections: two gopas and the drinking calf at the water's edge are blue Kanhas in the Yamuna.
3. A gopa with a peacock feather in his topknot; a gopa holding Kanha's flute; a cow with a feather behind her ear.
4. A calf with a tiny Vaishnava tilak on her forehead.
5. Brahma peeking from the cloud (not a Krishna: he's the one who got it wrong; he trips up the counters).

Answer (pinned comment, later): **all of them.** Every boy and every calf on that bank was Krishna.

**Sound:** a light loopable bed: the river, birds, calf bells, a giggle at the laddoo toss, one soft moo. A trending
sound goes on in Instagram.

## Facts used
- Krishna and the cowherd boys picnic on the soft sand bank of the Yamuna, sitting in rows and circles round Krishna,
  eating off leaves, flowers and bark as plates (Bhagavata Purana 10.13.6–10).
- Krishna in the middle, his flute tucked in his waistband, his horn and stick under his arm, a morsel in his left hand
  (BhP 10.13.11).
- The calves wander off grazing; Brahma hides the calves and then the boys (BhP 10.13.12–15).
- Krishna becomes every calf and every boy, exactly: their bodies, hands and feet, sticks, horns, flutes, ornaments,
  clothes, natures, names and ages ("sarvaṁ viṣṇumayaṁ"), and no one knows for a year (BhP 10.13.18–19, 10.13.27).
- Brahma comes back, sees both the hidden and the present boys and calves, can't tell which are real, and is bewildered
  (BhP 10.13.40–45). Then he sees every calf and boy as Vishnu, dark blue, in yellow silk (BhP 10.13.46–49). This is
  what the reflections show, and what "Even BRAHMA got this wrong" refers to.
- The tilak, feathers and flute on the others are clues, not plot claims: the text says they were all Krishna.
