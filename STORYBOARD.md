everyone knows. The never-knew-that payoff is real: the linga still stands at **Gokarna**, its name means "cow's ear",
and the Maha Ganapati there has a dent on his head. The card sells Books 1–3 (set of 3 for ₹500).

## Facts used
- The Gokarna Mahatmya and the popular retellings: Ravana won the Atmalinga from Shiva on the condition that it would
  stay wherever it was first set down. The devas feared it in Lanka. Ganesha came as a boy (a cowherd or a young
  brahmachari, by version), took it to hold, called Ravana three times and set it down. Ravana's pull twisted it into
  the shape of a cow's ear (go-karna), and he struck the boy on the head; the Maha Ganapati idol at Gokarna has a
  dent on its head.
- The reason Ravana had to stop differs by version: in the Shiva Purana line, Vishnu hides the sun so he must do his
  evening prayers; in the popular telling the reel uses, Varuna gets into his water so nature calls. The reel shows
  Varuna only as a hidden gag and says "the gods" were worried, not who did what.
