# Ramayan · Part 9: "Hanuman's Leap" (ad 44 · the Ramayan series)

Part 9 of the 12-part Ramayan series (format in ad 33's storyboard). Painted, **no voice**, like Parts 2–8: Bangers
captions carry the story (timeline and captions in `src/scenes/rp9_tl.js`), the characters talk with moving mouths, and
a retro cartoon SFX track sits under it (about -16 LUFS, fixed gain); the only music is a soft tanpura under Sita in the
grove. About 77 s. Made very cute, as Parts 6–8 were: Mount Mahendra squashed like a sponge with springs squirting out, the
golden mountain Mainaka with a smiley face, Hanuman getting bigger while Surasa's mouth gets wider, then shrinking tiny
and zipping in and out, his shadow grabbed so he pedals in mid-air, a cat-sized Hanuman on tiptoe, kissing his tail and
dancing over the wrong queen, and Ram's ring sparkling in Sita's hands.

The hook: Hanuman flew INTO a monster's mouth… on purpose (frame 0 is Surasa's open mouth with tiny Hanuman flying at
it). The pay-off comes in shot E. The ask on the card: "Comment JAI HANUMAN 🙏". The books line has no sale from now on
(owner, 24 Oct): "Our picture books · rishikatha.com · link in bio".

Not claimed: Jambavan rousing Hanuman (Kishkindha 66) and Lankini at the gate (Sundara 3), both left out for time; the
rakshasis around Sita; how many times the growing went round (the reel shows two rounds, the text names more). "Monster"
stands for the hideous rakshasi form Surasa took (Sundara 1.147). Simhika's end is kept gentle (she sinks; the text says
he tore her inside). Performance only: Mahendra's squash, the squirting springs, the birds and the snake popping out,
Mainaka's face and its "rest on me" smile (the text has the mountain speak in a man's form on its peak), Surasa's
eyelashes and blush, the "?" when he zips in, Hanuman pedalling in the air, the swirl eyes as Simhika sinks, the tiptoe,
the hearts and notes, Sita's tears of joy.
