# Kanha vs Radha #1: The butter thief

Cel style (CEL_GUIDE.md), the first episode of the series in RADHA_KANHA_SERIES.md. 15.6 s, 1080 × 1920, one fixed frame
like a comic panel: a plain cream ground, chibi Kanha and Radha side by side, handwritten lines (Kalam) in the speaker's
colour at the top of the safe band, no voice, a babble SFX track. Running gag of the series: Radha always wins.

Inspired by the format of an Instagram skit (two chibi kids on white, one line at a time, a punchline from a lila). The
characters, colours, lettering and gags are our own.

## Facts used

- Bhagavata Purana 10.8.29: the gopis complain to Yashoda that Krishna steals curd and milk, shares it out among the
  monkeys, and breaks the pot if a monkey won't eat (markaṭān bhokṣyan vibhajati sa cen nātti bhāṇḍaṁ bhinatti).
- Bhagavata Purana 10.9.8: Yashoda finds him standing on an upturned mortar, giving the butter to a monkey.
- Radha, Kanha's beloved companion in Vraja: Brahmavaivarta Purana (Krishna Janma Khanda), Garga Samhita, Jayadeva's
  Gita Govinda. She is not named in the Bhagavata, which some viewers may point out.
- What the reel invents (performance only, no plot claim): Radha being there, the brag, the monkey's denial, the offered
  butter. Kanha's butter-smeared face is the traditional image of Makhan Chor.
