# Ramayan · Part 6: "The Golden Deer" (ad 38 · the Ramayan series)

Part 6 of the 12-part Ramayan series (format in ad 33's storyboard). Painted, **no voice** (owner, 23 Oct: the chibi
reels without a VO do best): Bangers captions carry the story (timeline and captions in `src/scenes/rp6_tl.js`), the
characters talk with moving mouths, and a retro cartoon SFX track sits under it (about -16 LUFS, fixed gain); the only
music is a soft tanpura under Sita alone at the end. 78.5 s. Made extra cute (owner, 23 Oct: "extremely cute, viral"):
the deer gets big glossy eyes, a blush and a wink, Maricha's teeth chatter, the boy Ram's arrow sends him bobbing far out
at sea, Lakshman's flat "NO.", a wrong-answer buzzer on the Lakshman Rekha. The hook: that cute golden deer was a demon.
The myth-buster: there is no Lakshman Rekha in Valmiki's Ramayan (a line drawn on the ground, crossed out in red). The
cliffhanger: the holy man's shadow on the hut has ten heads.

## Facts used (Valmiki Ramayana; valmikiramayan.net numbering)
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| Ravana hears of Khara's fall (from Akampana, then Surpanakha) | Aranya 31–34 |
| He goes to Maricha, who is terrified of Ram | Aranya 35–39 |
| As a boy (at Vishwamitra's yagna) Ram's arrow hurled Maricha a hundred yojanas ("100 YOJANAS") into the ocean | Bala 30.15–19; Maricha recalls it, Aranya 38 |
| Ravana threatens to kill him; Maricha: better to die by Ram than by you | Aranya 40–41 |
| He becomes a golden deer with silver spots and antlers tipped with gems | Aranya 42 |
| Sita sees it and asks Ram to catch it | Aranya 43 |
| Lakshman warns it is Maricha in disguise | Aranya 43 |
| Ram leaves Lakshman to guard Sita and chases it far into the forest; it keeps vanishing and appearing | Aranya 43–44 |
| His arrow strikes; the dying Maricha cries in Ram's voice "Ha Sita! Ha Lakshmana!" | Aranya 44 |
| Sita begs Lakshman to go; he refuses; her harsh words force him to leave | Aranya 45 |
| He leaves her in the care of the forest deities: no line is drawn (the Lakshman Rekha is from later tellings) | Aranya 45 |
| Ravana comes to the hermitage disguised as a mendicant (parivrajaka) | Aranya 46 (Part 7) |

Not claimed: the words of Sita's accusation (the captions say only "her words were so harsh"); Ravana's chariot; the ten-headed
shadow is a picture of who the holy man is (Aranya 46 names him), not a scene from the text. Performance only: the
looming horned shadow and its red eyes, the deer's cute face and wink, Maricha's chattering teeth, his dizzy head bobbing
at sea, the thought bubble, the poofs as the deer hides, the sound rings of the cry, Lakshman's head shake, the red
cross and buzzer, the shadow.
