## Facts used
- **"Eight forms" is not a scriptural list.** Raj, Nyayadhish, Tapasvi, Yoddha, Saumya, Bal, Chhaya and Dandadhikari are
  the painter's names for aspects of Shani. So the reel says "a devotee painted the 8 faces", and claims no scripture
  for the eight.
- **Chhaya:** Sanjna, Surya's wife, couldn't bear his heat and left her shadow-likeness Chhaya in her place. Chhaya bore
  Shanaishchara (Markandeya Purana 77–78; Vishnu Purana 3.2; Harivamsha 1.9). "Karma follows like a shadow" is a
  metaphor (the painter's), not a claim.
- **Shanaishchara / Sade Sati:** the name means "the slow mover". Saturn takes ~29.5 years to go round the Sun, about
  2.5 years in each rashi, so his passage over the Moon sign and the signs on either side (Sade Sati) lasts ~7.5 years.
  (Jyotish; the orbit is astronomy.)
- **Nyayadhish / Karmaphal Data:** the giver of the fruits of karma. This is a devotional title and belief, worded as
  such ("No bribe. No favourite."). No plot claim.
- **Tapasvi:** Shani, a devotee of Krishna, was absorbed in meditation when his wife came to him. Ignored, she cursed
  him that whatever he looked at would be destroyed (Brahma Vaivarta Purana, Ganapati Khanda 11, as in ad 21).
- **Bal Shani:** the painter's "his first glance caused a solar eclipse" is NOT in the puranas (folk and TV-serial
  telling); it is left out. Used instead: Shiva Purana, Shatarudra Samhita 24–25: Pippalada makes Shani promise not
  to afflict anyone under 16 (ch. 25 v.15–18, checked on wisdomlib for ad 23's research).
- **Yoddha:** Shani's classical icon holds a bow, arrows and a trident (Matsya Purana's graha iconography, as summarised
  on Wikipedia "Shani"). "He fights for those who walk straight" is devotional belief, no plot claim.
- **Saumya:** Padma Purana: King Dasharatha confronted Shani to stop him crossing Rohini, then praised him (the
  Dasharatha Shani Stotra). Shani granted the boon and blessed those who recite the stotra.
- **Raj Shani:** Shasha Yoga, one of the five Pancha Mahapurusha yogas (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra), forms when
  Saturn is in its own sign (Capricorn, Aquarius) or exalted (Libra) in a kendra, and gives authority and lasting
  success.
- The mantra on the spread: ॐ शं शनैश्चराय नमः, the common Shani beej mantra. The score's choir chants "Om
  Shanaishcharaya Namah".
- The art is the painter's own, shown as he made it (the Chhaya painting's headless rider is kept, at the owner's
  choice). Nothing is redrawn.
