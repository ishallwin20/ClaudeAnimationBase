## Facts used
- **Lakshmi's vahana is the owl (uluka)**: her iconography, strongest in Bengal (the white barn owl, *lokkhi pencha*).
- **Diwali's Lakshmi Puja falls on Kartika Amavasya**, the new-moon night: the darkest night of the month. **Lakshmi and
  Ganesha are worshipped together** that night; Ganesha's vahana is the mouse (Mushika).
- **Day 1, Dhanteras: a lamp for Yama** (Yama Deepdaan, lit facing south at dusk on Kartika Krishna Trayodashi; the
  verse "kārttikasyāsite pakṣe trayodaśyāṃ niśāmukhe / yamadīpaṃ bahir dadyād apamṛtyur vinaśyati" is cited from the
  Skanda Purana). Yama's vahana is the water buffalo.
- **Day 2, Naraka Chaturdashi (Choti Diwali)**: Krishna rode Garuda with Satyabhama to Pragjyotisha and killed
  Narakasura (Bhagavata Purana 10.59.1–22).
- **Day 4, Govardhan Puja**: Krishna had Vraja worship Govardhan Hill, the cows and the brahmanas instead of Indra,
  feeding the cows and walking round the hill with them (Bhagavata Purana 10.24.25–30).
- **Day 5, Bhai Dooj (Yama Dwitiya)**: Yama visits his sister Yamuna, who feeds him at her home; he blesses brothers
  who eat at their sisters' homes that day (the Yama Dwitiya katha told for Bhai Dooj).
- Lakshmi's open palm pours gold coins (her iconography). Owls are night birds with huge, light-sensitive eyes, and
  can turn their heads about 270°. "Ullu" (owl) means a fool in Hindi slang.
- Made up, and obviously so: the union, the strike, the bonus, the broom, the sunglasses, the cow's attitude.
