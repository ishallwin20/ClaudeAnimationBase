# "Lakshmi Puja is TOMORROW. SAVE this: the whole puja, step by step." (ad 83 · 3D)

A 110 s save-bait explainer in the **3D** style (owner, 7 Nov 2026: "how to do the whole Diwali Puja correctly", from the
owner's own family's 11 steps, fact-checked, with Claude's additions; English only; full detail ~100 s; Girish VO;
murti order made the ask; havan kept, marked optional). Built the day before Lakshmi Puja (Sun 8 Nov 2026).

**The idea:** a numbered checklist people save and follow tonight/tomorrow. A home mandir at night in brass and
marigolds; each step is a shot with a big STEP N in the top band. The ask is a real household difference: *who sits
where on your chowki?* (owner's order, as you face them: Saraswati, Lakshmi, Ganesha). Save prompt in the hook and at
the end. Navadurga card (no price). Matte look: no fx bloom, cine.js bloom .14, DOF, a gentle warm grade.

## The steps (owner's list + additions)
Owner's: clean utensils, diyas, murtis · the order (S, L, G from the left) · new clothes, mala · place them · flowers ·
kalash (water, coin, mango leaves, coconut, tika) · tika, prasad, flowers with mantras · Ganesha first · havan · aarti
(Ganesha, Lakshmi) · *mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ* forgiveness.
Added (Claude, owner said "definitely add"): the muhurat (Pradosh Kaal), the red cloth on the chowki, clay murtis
wiped not washed, supari/rice/swastik/mauli on the kalash, sitting facing east or north, achaman, the sankalp,
pushpanjali, diyas through the house, rangoli and footprints at the door, a lamp all night; a lotus for Lakshmi,
kheel-batashe as bhog. Not used (time): Kuber and the account books, Navagraha/Shodasha-matrika puja, pradakshina.

## Facts used
| claim in the reel | source |
|---|---|
| Lakshmi Puja is Sun 8 Nov 2026, after sunset in Pradosh Kaal; Delhi 5:54–7:50 PM; check your city | Kartika Amavasya prevails in Pradosh Kaal on 8 Nov; Drik Panchang's New Delhi muhurat 5:54–7:50 PM, Pradosh 5:31–8:09 PM (quoted by [hindutone](https://hindutone.com/festivals/lakshmi-puja-muhurat-2026-india-city-wise/), [smartpuja](https://www.smartpuja.com/blog/diwali-2026-date-lakshmi-puja-muhurat-vidhi/)); city windows differ, so the tag names Delhi and the VO says "check your city's muhurat". Padma Purana Uttara 122 places Lakshmi's worship on the new-moon night (as in ad 58) |
| Metal murtis get a bath; clay murtis just a gentle wipe | practice: unbaked/painted clay dissolves in water; metal and stone murtis are bathed (snana). Stated as practice |
| A red cloth on a clean chowki | the common Lakshmi-puja vidhi (a raised seat with red or yellow cloth) ([panchangbodh](https://panchangbodh.com/diwali-puja-vidhi), [exoticindia](https://exoticindiaart.com/blog/diwali-ritual-the-significance-lakshmi-ganesh-puja)) |
| As you face them: Saraswati, Lakshmi, Ganesha; "many homes do it this way, yours may differ" | no scripture fixes it; guides disagree on left/right (Ganesha to Lakshmi's left is the most cited, which is the owner's order seen from the front); some homes put Ganesha on the viewer's left. Hence "many homes" and the ask |
| New clothes, a mala, flowers; a lotus for Lakshmi | vastra, mala and pushpa are standard upacharas; the lotus is Lakshmi's flower (she is *padmā*, *kamalā*) |
| The kalash: water, a coin, supari and rice inside; five mango leaves, a coconut in red cloth, a kumkum swastik, a red mauli thread | the common kalash-sthapana (purna-kumbha) practice; ad 82 used the same kalash |
| Sit facing east or north | the worshipper faces east (*prāṅmukha*) or north (*udaṅmukha*): the standard puja-paddhati instruction |
| Sip water three times: achaman | the three sips (*ācamana*) with Keshava's names that open a puja; shown, names not said |
| Water, a few grains of rice and a flower in the hand; say your name, the day and why you pray: the sankalp | the sankalpa formula (name, gotra, place, date/tithi, purpose), taken with water, akshat and a flower in the right hand |
| Always begin with Ganesha; in the Shiva Purana the gods made him the first to be worshipped | Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Kumara Khanda 18.23–25: the gods grant that he is worshipped first, and Shiva worships him first ([wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226138.html)) |
| Then each god in turn: tilak, akshat, flowers, incense, a lamp and bhog, with their mantra | gandha, akshata, pushpa, dhupa, dipa, naivedya: the core upacharas (panchopachara + akshata). Mantras on screen: ॐ गं गणपतये नमः, ॐ श्रीं महालक्ष्म्यै नमः, ॐ ऐं सरस्वत्यै नमः (the common bija nama-mantras); kheel-batashe as Diwali bhog is custom |
| If your family does a havan, it comes now | optional; many homes skip it on Diwali night. Shown with the ad 49 kund |
| Aarti: Ganesha first, then Lakshmi | follows Ganesha-first; the household order of the two aartis |
| Flowers in folded hands: pushpanjali | the closing pushpanjali upachara |
| The forgiveness prayer, *mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ sureśvara / yat pūjitaṁ mayā deva paripūrṇaṁ tad astu me* | the traditional closing kshama-prarthana (also verse 3 of the Devi Kshama Prarthana after the Durga Saptashati, and in Shiva kshama stotras); no single purana claimed, so the tag says "the traditional closing prayer". Gloss: "Lacking mantra, rite or devotion, O Lord, let what I offered be complete" |
| Light diyas in every room, rangoli and footprints at the door; many homes keep one lamp burning all night | lamps on Diwali night (Padma Uttara 122 / Skanda Kartika Mahatmya); rangoli, Lakshmi's footprints and the all-night lamp are customs: hence "many homes" |

Not said (kept out on purpose): any claim that one order of murtis is the only right one; "standing murtis are
inauspicious"; vastu directions for the room; a specific purana for the kshama verse; Kuber/bahi-khata (time).
Performance only (no claim): the brass murtis' look (Ganesha with ankusha, pasha, modaks and his mouse; Lakshmi with
lotuses and coins; Saraswati with veena, rosary and book), the coins at "complete", the petal shower, the wash bench.
