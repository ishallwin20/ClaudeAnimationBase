# Fall asleep with Kanha (Scribble style, 30 s seamless loop)

Part of the Scribble "Breathe with…" set (after reel/breathe-hanuman). Made only in code: no AI images, no photo.

## The loop
- A sleepy breath, three rounds of 10 s: **Breathe in** (4 s, "slowly…") and **Breathe out** (6 s, "slowly… let the
  day go"). Softer, smaller words than the other reels. Frame 0 shows "Breathe in".
- On the bank of the Yamuna at night (out of focus: the moon, kadamba trees, the river with the moon on it, a few lamps
  in a far village) Kanha has fallen asleep leaning on a calf, his flute across his lap, a peacock feather in his hair.
  The calf's flank and Kanha's chest rise together; fireflies blink round them; a grass verge in front.
- Sound (`tools/kanhasleep_sfx.mjs` → `assets/kanhasleep_sfx.wav`): a soft tanpura, a slow bansuri lullaby in Bhupali
  (rising on the in-breath, falling on the out-breath, a little different each round), crickets, the river, a firefly
  chime now and then. Loops.

## Facts used
- Krishna and Balarama herded the calves of Vraja as boys (vatsapāla): Bhagavata Purana 10.11.37–38. Kanha asleep on a
  calf is a picture, not a claimed episode.
- A longer out-breath than in-breath, offered as a way to wind down; no medical claims.
