# Breathe with Nandi (Scribble style, 30 s seamless loop)

Part of the Scribble "Breathe with…" set (after reel/breathe-hanuman). Made only in code: no AI images, no photo.

## The loop
- Even breathing, three rounds of 10 s: **Inhale** (5 s, पूरक) and **Exhale** (5 s, रेचक), no holds. Frame 0 already
  shows "Inhale"; the last frame ends the third out-breath.
- Nandi lies facing us as he faces the sanctum: a white bull, forelegs folded, hump behind his head, a red cloth with a
  gold border over his back, a marigold garland and a brass bell, a tripundra of ash and a kumkum dot, eyes closed. He
  lies on a stone platform in a temple courtyard at dusk, out of focus behind him the gold flagstaff and the gopuram,
  lamps along the walls. Two brass diyas burn in front of him.
- Breathing in, his whole body swells and his head lifts; breathing out he settles. An ear flicks now and then.
- Sound (`tools/nandibreath_sfx.mjs` → `assets/nandibreath_sfx.wav`): a tanpura, a far temple bell at each in-breath, a
  slow bull's breath in and out, a little bell on his neck at each out-breath, a jingle when an ear flicks, dusk crickets.

## Facts used
- Nandi sits facing the sanctum (the linga) in Shiva temples, between the flagstaff (dhwajastambha) and the shrine: the
  standard temple layout, so the gopuram and the flagstaff are behind him.
- Equal counts in and out is what yoga classes call sama vritti ("equal breath"); the caption doesn't claim it's ancient.
- No medical claims.
