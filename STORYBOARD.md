# Ad 89: "The night HANUMAN almost GAVE UP" (Hanuman series, Part 4)

A 90.2 s Painted reel with Bangers captions, no voiceover, retro cartoon SFX and music only as mild beds (a low pad
under the night, a drone and harp under Sita, a warm swell and a harp glissando for the hug, a music box under the
ask). Part 4 of the 10-part Hanuman Ji series (`HANUMAN_SERIES.md` on `main`); it stands alone, and ends on the
no-price Navadurga card.

```
Logline: In Lanka, Hanuman thought he had found Sita: he kissed his own tail and climbed the pillars. It was
         Mandodari. Then he searched every house, twice, and found nothing; if he went home empty-handed, Ram would
         not live, and one grief would knock down the next. He decided never to go back, or to give up his life.
         Then: "one who stays alive sees good things." One garden was left. Sita was in it. Back home, Ram had
         nothing to give him but a hug: "it is all I have."
Why it should travel: a comic cold open (tail kiss, pillar climb, wrong queen), a feeling everyone has had (giving
         up), two lines worth saving (Sundara 12.10, "not losing heart is the root of all good", shown in Devanagari;
         13.47, "one who stays alive sees good things"), the dominoes as a picture of dread, and a tender twist.
Hook:    Frame 0: Hanuman alone on Lanka's wall at night, a rain cloud over his head, a small lamp beside him.
         Caption up: "THE NIGHT HANUMAN" / "ALMOST GAVE UP" (gold). 1.2 a tear; 2.4 a long sigh.
Arc:     sad (hook) → sneaky → overjoyed → embarrassed → tired → resolved → tired again → dread → despair →
         resolve → found her → held.
Motif:   the little lamp: low beside him on the wall, guttering in despair, flaring at "one who stays alive",
         burning bright in the ask.
```

## Facts used
**Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda** (verse numbers as at valmikiramayan.net/utf8/sundara/sarga10–15, read 13 Nov 2026):
- 10.11–12: he sees Ravana asleep on a shining couch, goes near, and moves away in dread, "as from a hissing serpent".
- 10.50–51: among the women, a very beautiful one asleep on a couch set apart: Mandodari.
- 10.53: from her beauty and youth he reasons "this is Sita", and is overjoyed.
- 10.54: he slapped his arms, kissed his tail, rejoiced, played, sang, paced, climbed the pillars and dropped to the
  ground, showing his monkey nature.
- 11.1–3: he puts the thought away: Sita, parted from Ram, would not sleep, eat, adorn herself or drink; this is
  another woman.
- 12.1–6: he searches the creeper houses, picture halls and night chambers; no Sita; "my effort is wasted".
- 12.10–11: "Not losing heart (anirveda) is the root of prosperity, the highest happiness; it moves every
  undertaking. I will search again the places not yet seen."
- 12.14–19: he searches again: cellars, houses at crossroads, wells, lakes, opening doors and going in; not a space
  of four finger-widths left unsearched (12.17); still not Sita.
- 12.23–25: seeing his effort and the crossing of the ocean wasted, he is downcast again.
- 13.23–24: "If I go and say 'Sita is not found', Ram will give up his life."
- 13.25–37: then Lakshman, Bharat, Shatrughna, Kausalya, Sumitra, Kaikeyi, Sugriva, Ruma, Tara, Angada and the
  vanaras, one after another.
- 13.38: "I will not go back to Kishkindha."
- 13.40–43: "I will live here as a hermit by the sea, or make a pyre and enter the fire, or fast to death."
- 13.47: "There are many evils in dying; one who lives obtains good things. So I will keep my life."
- 13.52, 13.55: "I will search Lanka again and again; this Ashoka garden has not been searched by me."
- 14.40–52: in the garden he climbs a shimshapa tree and hides in its leaves; 15.18–30: he sees Sita, unadorned,
  in tears, sitting on the bare ground; 17.18: "below that tree".

**Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 1** (valmikiramayan.net/utf8/yuddha/sarga1):
- 1.12: "it pains me that I cannot do a fitting kindness to him who brought this good news."
- 1.13: "this embrace, which is all I have (sarvasva-bhūta), I give to the great-souled Hanuman."
- 1.14: Ram, his limbs thrilling with joy, embraced him.

Not claimed: anything about why Ravana slept sitting (he lay on his couch, 10.11; the reel sits him up so his ten
heads read), the women around Ravana (10.31–49, left out), Hanuman's size in each shot.
Performance only (no plot claims): the snort, the rain cloud, the lamp (the text has four gold lamps in Ravana's
chamber, 10.25, but none beside Hanuman), the doors, the well and the pot gags (the text names wells and houses,
12.15–18, not pots), the domino tiles (a picture of 13.25–37, not a quote), Sita in the bubble, the leap into the
garden, Ram looking at his empty hands, the hearts.
