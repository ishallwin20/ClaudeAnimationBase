# Ramayan · Part 11: "Ram vs the Ocean" (ad 46 · the Ramayan series)

Part 11 of the 12-part Ramayan series (format in ad 33's storyboard). Painted, **no voice**, like Parts 2–10: Bangers
captions carry the story (timeline and captions in `src/scenes/rp11_tl.js`), the characters talk with moving mouths,
retro cartoon SFX under it (about -16 LUFS, fixed gain); the only music is a soft tanpura under the three days of
waiting. 58.0 s. Made cute: the monkeys hopping mad at the "spy", their jaws dropping at "even Ravana!", the DAY 1-2-3
pill as sun and moon wheel over Ram, fish and a crab leaping out of the churned sea in a panic, the Ocean rising with
his hands joined, Nala popping up with a thumbs-up, little vanaras trotting boulders along the growing bridge with a
DAY 1…5 pill, the army marching across, Ravana's ten heads glaring from the wall.

The hook: The Ocean ignored Ram for 3 days… BAD IDEA (frame 0: Ram at the shore, the bow drawn at the sea; it rhymes
with Part 10's tease). The ask on the card: "Comment JAI SHRI RAM 🙏". The books line: "Our picture books ·
rishikatha.com".

## Facts used (Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda; valmikiramayan.net numbering)

| Ram: I give protection to all who come to me; bring him, even if it were Ravana himself | Yuddha 18.33–34 |
| To cross the sea, Ram lies on the grass by the shore and prays to the Ocean for three nights | Yuddha 21.1–10 |
| The Ocean does not appear | Yuddha 21.11 |
| Angry, Ram shoots arrows that churn the sea and its creatures | Yuddha 21.24–30 |
| He takes up the Brahmastra | Yuddha 22.4–6 |

| The arrow, once fitted, has to be loosed; Ram shoots it to the north (Drumakulya) | Yuddha 22.29–35 |
| The Ocean: Nala, son of Vishwakarma, can build a bridge, and I will bear it | Yuddha 22.43–47 |
| Nala and the monkeys build it, a hundred yojanas long, in five days | Yuddha 22.50–72 |
| The army crosses to Lanka | Yuddha 22.73–80 |
| The finale tease: Ram and Ravana | Yuddha 107–108 (Part 12) |

Not claimed (later retellings, not Valmiki): stones floating because Ram's name was written on them; the squirrel. Left
out for time: Vibhishana crowned with sea water (19), the desert at Drumakulya (22.36–41). "Prayed sitting" stands for
Ram lying on the grass facing the sea. Performance only: the hopping, the jaws, the DAY pills, the fish and crab, the
glow on the Brahmastra, Nala's thumbs-up, the boulders carried overhead, Ravana's heads glaring.
