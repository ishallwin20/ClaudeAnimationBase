# Breathe with Kumbhakarna (Scribble style, 28 s seamless loop, a gentle comedy)

Part of the Scribble "Breathe with…" set (after reel/breathe-hanuman). Made only in code: no AI images, no photo.

## The loop
- Two rounds of 14 s: **Inhale** (4 s, पूरक), **Hold** (4 s, "कुम्भक (not Kumbhakarna)"), **Exhale** (6 s, रेचक).
  A fixed line under the words: "He slept six months at a time. / We'll just do 28 seconds." Frame 0 shows "Inhale".
- Kumbhakarna lies asleep on his back on a crimson mattress in his palace hall at night (out of focus: arched windows of
  moonlight, torches on the pillars), head on a gold bolster, face turned to us, mukut askew, a hand on his huge belly.
- In: a long rattling snore, his mouth opening wide, his belly rising; the brass lamp's flame is pulled toward him.
  Hold: everything waits; the myna riding his belly hops and turns round. Out: his lips purse, the moustache flutters,
  a whistle, the flame is blown flat, little z's drift up.
- Sound (`tools/kumbhabreath_sfx.mjs` → `assets/kumbhabreath_sfx.wav`): the snore, silence and the myna's chirps and hop,
  the whistle over a soft "pffff", a very soft tanpura so it still belongs to the series.

## Facts used
- Kumbhakarna slept for six months at a time and woke for one day, by Brahma's word: Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 61
  (Vibhishana tells Ram his story). The joke line says only that.
- Kumbhaka (holding the breath) and Kumbhakarna ("pot-eared") are only a sound-alike; the line says "not Kumbhakarna" and
  claims nothing.
- The myna, the lamp and the z's are gags that claim no plot fact. No medical claims.
