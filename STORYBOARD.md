# Ad 19: "Maa DURGA's name came from a DEMON." (Cinematic · Books 1–3 · 73.2 s)

A captioned retelling in the **Cinematic** style ([CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md](CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md)) of the Durgama story from the
Devi Bhagavata Purana: how the Devi became Shatakshi, Shakambhari and, by killing the demon Durgama, **Durga**. There is
no voice (the 1M-view Ravana reel had none). The captions tell it, synthesized SFX carry the hits, and a Lyria 3 Pro score
sits underneath. It ends on the Books 1–3 card (set of 3 for ₹500) with the Follow line.

```
Logline: The demon Durgama pleased Brahma and asked for ALL the Vedas. The rishis forgot every mantra, the yagnas stopped,
         and no rain fell for a hundred years. The rishis prayed in the Himalayas, and the Devi came with countless eyes
         (Shatakshi). Every eye wept, for nine nights, and her tears became rivers; she fed the world from her own hands
         (Shakambhari). Durgama came with a thousand armies. She put the gods and rishis inside a ring of light and stood
         outside, alone. Goddesses burst out of her body; in ten days his armies were gone. On the eleventh day she drew
         fifteen arrows: his horses, his charioteer, his flag, his eyes, his arms, and five through his heart. She gave the
         Vedas back and said: "I killed Durgama, so my name is DURGA."
Why it should travel: "known yet unknown" (the most famous name, a story almost nobody knows), a mother who weeps for
         nine nights before she fights, a fight with a countable climax (fifteen arrows counted on the string, the score's
         hush and hit), and a NAME call-out (the best comment lever: ad 15 got 300 comments with one).
Comment: "Is your name DURGA, SHATAKSHI or SHAKAMBHARI? Comment it!" Pinned: "Where did the 15 arrows go? Count them:
         4 horses, 1 charioteer, 1 flag, 2 eyes, 2 arms, 5 heart 🏹"
```

## Facts used
Every plot beat is from the Devi Bhagavata Purana, Book 7, ch. 28 ("the glory of Shatakshi/Shakambhari"; Swami
Vijnanananda's translation, sacred-texts.com/hin/db/bk07ch28.htm), foretold in the Devi Mahatmya (Markandeya Purana),
11.46–50.

text says nine nights). The eye aura is how the reel draws "innumerable eyes"; the weapons in her eight hands are the
gods' gifts from the Mahishasura story (DM 2), used here only as her look.

## Files
- Scene: `src/scenes/durgama.js` (times in `TL`), props `src/scenes/durgama_props.js` (Durgama's palette, the mantras in
  flight, dry cracks and the riverbed, rain, Shatakshi's `eyeAura`, `produce`, `sprouts`, `army`, `ringOfLight`,
  `shakti`, the `archer` silhouette and `arrowLight`, `banner`).
- New shared Cinematic cast (on `main`): `devi({ durga })` (eight arms: Kala's sword, Vayu's bow, the Ocean's lotus,
  Indra's vajra, Vishnu's chakra, Varuna's conch), `deviHead({ tears })`, `arunasura({ pose: 'stand', arms: 'roar' })`;
  sheet panels 38–40 and `docs/cine_cast_durgama.jpg`.
- Sound: `tools/durgama_sfx.mjs` → `assets/durgama_sfx.wav`, then the mix → `assets/durgama_mix.wav`: the score at .42 on
  a level curve (.55 under the drought, .8–.9 under the eyes and tears), faded out at 69.6, the SFX on top, loudnorm −15 LUFS.
- The score: `tools/durgama_score.txt`, three `google/lyria-3-pro-preview` takes asked for at once. Take 1 (74.6 s) is
  used as it came: a hit at 0, the hush at 52.2–54.0 and its biggest hit at 56.0, so the bow is drawn in the hush and the
  five arrows land on the hit. Take 2 ran 98 s with 2 s of silence at the top; take 3 opened silent.
