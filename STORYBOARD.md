# "Was RAM right to leave SITA?" (ad 67 · Books 1–3 · Cinematic + 3D)

A ~84 s reel in the **Cinematic** style with the **3D** India map as its spine. The hook is the most argued question in
the Ramayan; the reel takes no side. Four poets across two thousand years testify, each in their own words, each
pinned on the map where they wrote: **Valmiki** (Ram's reason, and his apology), **Kalidasa** (Sita's message to the
king, and her prayer), **Bhavabhuti** (who reunites them), **Tulsidas** (who never tells the exile). The thesis is
Valmiki's own line: at Sita's oath the crowd was divided ("well done, Sita", "well done, Ram", and some said both).
The ask is an identity call-out, not a vote: "Which Ramayan did YOU grow up with?", with the regional Ramayans lit on
the map. English VO: narrator `girish_en_devotional` (pace 1.25); Sita's three lines `aditi_en_stories` (pace
1.05–1.1, a light echo). Ram stays a silhouette throughout: the picture never judges his face. Card: Books 1–3, set of
3 for ₹500.

## Facts used
Valmiki is quoted from the Critical Edition (Baroda; GRETIL `sa_rAmAyaNa`), Uttara Kanda; the Gita Press vulgate
numbers these sargas a few later (the oath is its 97, the golden Sita its 99). Kalidasa from GRETIL
`sa_kAlidAsa-raghuvaMza`.

Performance only (no claim): the map and its pillars, the whispering silhouettes, the river at dusk and the boat, the
verse rising, the moon over the palace, the light in the cracks, the golden statue's glow, the crowd lit in two halves.
Not used: the washerman (a later version, Padma Purana / folk), Ravana's portrait (folk), any verdict.

## Voiceover (tools/sita_vo.json; captions are the script)
```
hook     Was RAM right to leave SITA?
setup    We won't judge. FOUR POETS will.
valmiki  First, VALMIKI. Ayodhya whispered: / "RAVANA held her. If the king takes her back, / we'll have to bear it from our wives too."
ram1     RAM told his brothers: "My heart knows she is PURE. / But for fear of such blame, I'd give up my own LIFE."
kali     In KALIDASA, Sita sends the king a message:
sita1    (Sita) "You saw me PURE in the FIRE. / Still, over gossip, you left me. / Is that worthy of your family?"
pray     And yet she prays:
sita2    (Sita) "Even in my next life, be my HUSBAND."
heart    Cast out of his house. Never out of his HEART.
yagna    Years later, at his YAGNA, Ram tells VALMIKI: / "I knew she was SINLESS. Forgive me."
oath     (Sita) "If I have never thought of anyone but RAM, / MOTHER EARTH, open for me."
earth    And the EARTH took her home.
gold     Ram never married again. At every yagna, / a SITA of GOLD stood beside him.
bhava    BHAVABHUTI couldn't bear it. / In his play, they are REUNITED.
tulsi    And TULSIDAS? In his Ramcharitmanas, / there is NO EXILE at all.
split    Even in Valmiki, the crowd was DIVIDED. / "Well done, SITA!" "Well done, RAM!" / And some said: BOTH.
ask      Which RAMAYAN did YOU grow up with? Comment yours.
```
Speech-to-text (saaras:v3) on every take: word for word after one fix ("next life too" was heard as "to": now "Even in
my next life").
