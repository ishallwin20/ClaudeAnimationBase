# The Third Eye Alarm Clock

A 32.5-second vertical reel (1080×1920, 24 fps, bpm 100) starring Shiva, Parvati Maa, Bal Bappa and Kartikeya.
Scene: [src/scenes/third_eye.js](src/scenes/third_eye.js), sets and props: [src/scenes/third_eye_props.js](src/scenes/third_eye_props.js),
characters: [src/shiva.js](src/shiva.js), [src/kartikeya.js](src/kartikeya.js), [src/parvati.js](src/parvati.js), [src/bal_bappa.js](src/bal_bappa.js),
sound effects: [tools/third_eye_sfx.mjs](tools/third_eye_sfx.mjs) → `assets/third_eye_sfx.wav`.

Logline: At dawn on Kailash, Bal Bappa and Kartikeya want Shiva to wake up and play, but nothing breaks his trance:
         not a spear on a shield, not his own damaru, and the third try nearly opens the third eye. Parvati Maa
         puts a cup of chai under his nose, and the third eye pops open.
Hook:    "Waking up the Destroyer of the Universe is a risky morning routine." (caption, 0.4–3.6)
Sting:   "Maa always knows the cheat code." (caption over the last wide)
World:   Kailash before sunrise: cool blue snow, lilac sky and Shiva's own blue. During the scare the sky turns
         ember red, then back. When the third eye pops open the sun comes up behind the peak and everything turns
         gold. Palette: TE in third_eye_props.js.
Motif:   the third eye, which starts closed with one faint ember, trembles red, then POPS; the film opens and closes on
         an eye-shaped iris. Vasuki (the snake) is the one who wakes up every time Shiva doesn't.
Arcs:    Kids:   excited → determined → hopeful → sheepish / dizzy → determined → scared → relieved → confused → starstruck → love
         Shiva:  serene (all film) → sniff → surprised (POP) → bliss → happy → love
         Parvati: amused → playful wink → smug (sips the chai herself)
