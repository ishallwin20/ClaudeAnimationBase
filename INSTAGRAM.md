Why does the Ganesha at GOKARNA have a DENT on his head? 🤕 Because VISHNU faked a sunset. 🌅

Ravana won Shiva's Atmalinga with ONE rule: wherever it first touches the ground, it stays forever. In Lanka, it would
make him unbeatable, so the gods panicked. As Ravana neared Gokarna, Vishnu hid the sun and made it look like dusk.

And Ravana never missed his evening prayers… but he couldn't pray holding the linga, and he couldn't put it down. 😅

A little brahmin boy (totally NOT Ganesha) offered to hold it: "I'll call you three times. Then I put it down." He
called… very fast. Down it went, and it never moved again. Ravana pulled with all twenty arms, and the linga took the
shape of a cow's ear: Go-karna. Furious, he struck the boy on the head, and the Maha Ganapati at Gokarna, Karnataka
still has the dent today. 🙏

(As told in the Gokarna legend, from the Skanda Purana tradition and the temple's own story.)
