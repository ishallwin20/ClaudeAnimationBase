Why does the Ganesha at GOKARNA have a DENT on his head? 🤕 Because RAVANA needed a toilet. 🚽

Ravana won Shiva's Atmalinga, Shiva's own soul in a stone, with ONE rule: wherever it touches the ground, it stays
forever. In Lanka, it would make Ravana unbeatable. So on the long walk home, the gods made him VERY thirsty…

Ten heads. One bladder. 😅

A little cowherd boy (totally NOT Ganesha) offered to hold it: "I'll call you three times, then I put it down." He
counted… very fast. Down it went, and it never moved again. Ravana pulled with all twenty arms, and it twisted like a
cow's ear: Go-karna. Furious, he bonked the boy on the head, and the Maha Ganapati at Gokarna, Karnataka still has the
dent today. 🙏
