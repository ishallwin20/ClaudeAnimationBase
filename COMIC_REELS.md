# Comic reels: Painted mini-series that sell the CHIRANJEEVI comics

Owner, 26 Nov 2026: small Painted reels with a very strong hook that point people to buy the comics. The marketing has
to be intentional and part of the story, not a card at the end. Comic plan: [CHIRANJEEVI.md](CHIRANJEEVI.md).

## The device (every episode)

1. **Hook in frame 0** with a question the comic answers. One subject, the caption already up.
2. **The Painted story** (30–35 s) tells one gripping stretch of the issue, faithfully (the retelling rules apply; use
   the comic's SCRIPT.md verses). A small orange pill, "FROM THE COMIC · CHIRANJEEVI #N", sits at the bottom of the safe
   band for the first ~9 s, so viewers know from the start that this is a comic.
3. **Stop at the worst moment.** The frame freezes, ink spreads over the painting, half a second of total silence, a
   boom, and the comic's own page for the very next beat fills the frame, then settles into a page.
4. **The pages swipe** like a phone (2 real pages from the paid part), then a **blurred, padlocked page**:
   "PAGE N OF M · ONLY IN THE COMIC".
5. **The cover rises**: "THE REEL STOPS HERE. / THE COMIC DOESN'T." · "CHIRANJEEVI · ISSUE N · OUT NOW" · the
   rishikatha.com pill · "Link in bio · Follow @therishikatha".

Why: Issue 1's site preview converted 6 of 140 visitors (20 Nov) because its first pages were mood. These reels are the
preview done right: a question first, an event every shot, and a stop at the cliffhanger with the comic as the only way
on.

Rules: standalone episodes, never "Part N" on screen (series decay); ~45–55 s; Painted, Bangers, synth SFX, no voice;
no price on screen until the owner confirms one; the comic pages shown come from after the cliffhanger. Pages are packed
for the reel with `node tools/comic_pages.mjs <issue> <pages> <blurred pages>` (from the issue's IG JPEG export).
